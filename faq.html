Yoga Mat Sourcing FAQ — Answered by the Factory

The real questions overseas buyers ask when sourcing yoga mats & fitness gear from China — MOQ, certification, customization, lead time and why a direct Fujian factory wins.

Sourcing & MOQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom yoga mats?
TPE yoga mats start at 100 pcs per design and color; foam rollers and EVA yoga blocks from 500 pcs; most accessories 500–1,000 pcs. Low MOQ lets new and small brands test the market with minimal inventory risk.
Can I order a small batch of yoga mats to test a new brand?
Yes. Our 100-pcs MOQ on TPE mats is designed for brand launches and market testing. You can start with one color and size, validate demand, then scale up.
Where are the best yoga mat manufacturers in China?
China's main yoga and fitness gear clusters are in Fujian (Jinjiang/Quanzhou), Zhejiang and Guangdong. Fujian, where SANFAN is based, is known for integrated sports-goods supply chains, fast sampling and export experience.
How do I find a reliable OEM yoga mat factory in China?
Look for a real manufacturing base (not a trader), verifiable certifications (ISO 9001, BSCI), export track record, transparent MOQ/lead time and a responsive team. SANFAN meets all of these — visit our factory anytime.
TPE vs PU/rubber yoga mat: which should my brand choose?
TPE is lightweight, eco-friendly and cost-effective — ideal for mass-market and private-label lines. PU/rubber offers premium grip for hot yoga and studios. We make both and help you choose by use case and price.
What is the difference between OEM, ODM and private label for yoga mats?
OEM = we produce to your spec/design. ODM = we provide existing designs you brand as yours. Private label = our standard products shipped under your brand with your logo and packaging. All three are available at SANFAN.

Quality & Certifications

Are your yoga mats non-toxic and eco-friendly?
Yes. Our TPE mats are odorless, recyclable and free of harmful plasticizers. Materials are selected for REACH and CA Prop 65 compliance so they are safe for daily use in any market.
What certifications do yoga mats need for the US/EU market?
For the US, CA Prop 65 and 6P/7P phthalate limits matter; for the EU, REACH (SVHC) compliance is required. SANFAN holds ISO 9001 and BSCI, and our materials are tested to these standards.
How do you keep quality consistent across production batches?
We run in-house QC with batch traceability: incoming material checks, in-process inspection and final AQL inspection before shipment. Consistent formulations and tooling keep every batch stable.
Are TPE yoga mats biodegradable?
TPE is more eco-friendly than PVC and is recyclable, though not soil-biodegradable. For brands prioritizing sustainability we offer recyclable TPE lines and can advise on eco messaging.
How slip-resistant are your mats for hot yoga or sweaty sessions?
PU/rubber mats give the strongest grip even when wet — our top choice for hot yoga. TPE double-layer mats offer reliable dry grip. We can match surface texture to your practice profile.

Customization

Can you print my logo on the yoga mats?
Yes. We offer logo printing, embossing and laser engraving on mats and most accessories, with color-matched packaging to build your brand identity.
What can be customized — size, thickness, color, double-color, packaging?
Nearly everything: material, size, thickness (4–12 mm), color, double-color, surface finish, logo, fold style and packaging. Just share your spec and we engineer it.
Can you make non-standard sizes such as 1830×610×3 mm, wide or double-wide?
Yes. Beyond standard 1830×610 mm we produce wide (80/122 cm) and double-wide formats, plus custom lengths and thicknesses. Send your dimensions and we confirm tooling.
Do you provide custom packaging, barcodes and inserts?
Yes. We design and produce custom boxes, belly bands, barcodes, QR codes and inserts, so your product arrives shelf-ready under your brand.
Can you develop a new product from my sample or design?
Absolutely. Our R&D team develops new items from your sample, sketch or concept — from material selection to final mass production, with a physical sample in about 7 days.

Lead Time & Shipping

How fast can I get a sample and bulk production?
Physical samples are ready in about 7 days with your logo, color and packaging. Bulk lead time depends on order size and customization; we confirm a clear timeline at quotation.
What is your monthly production capacity?
We produce 200,000+ yoga mats per month across 5 production lines, plus rollers, blocks, bags and apparel — enough to support both growing brands and large repeat orders.
Do you ship to the US, EU or Australia? Which incoterms (FOB, EXW, DDP)?
Yes, worldwide. We ship under FOB, EXW, CIF or DDP depending on your preference, and handle export documentation for smooth customs clearance.
Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA warehouses?
Yes. We prepare FBA-compliant labeling, cartons and paperwork and can ship directly to Amazon fulfillment centers in the US, EU and other regions.
