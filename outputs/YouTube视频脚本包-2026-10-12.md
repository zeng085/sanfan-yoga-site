# YouTube 视频脚本包（4 条 · GEO 优化版）

> 依据：Airefs 2026-10 实测 —— YouTube 是**87% 品牌**被 Google AI Overviews 引用的来源，
> 93% 引用落在**单个视频**，且视频**字幕被 AI 当文本源读取**。
> 项目文档另测：YouTube 提及与 AI 引用相关性 **0.737**，远高于外链 0.266。
> → 这四条不需要露脸、不需要额外产能：工厂实拍 + 英文旁白 + 硬字幕即可。

## 通用制作规范

| 项 | 设定 | 为什么 |
|---|---|---|
| 时长 | 2–3 分钟 | 短于此拿不到信息密度，长于此完播率掉 |
| 形式 | 实拍 + 画中画演示 + **硬字幕**（非自动生成） | 字幕是 AI 唯一能读的文字层 |
| 语言 | **英文旁白 + 英文字幕** | 全球 AI 引擎训练以英文为主 |
| 标题公式 | `[具体动作] + (括号内数字/时间)` | 括号内数字是 AI 摘取锚点 |
| 描述 | 前两行复述核心数字，末尾列关键词 | 描述区被索引 |
| 封面 | 大字 4 个词以内 + 实物对比 | — |
| 频道 | 个人/工厂号，简介写 `12 years in yoga mat manufacturing` | 实体一致性 |
| 标签 | yoga mat manufacturing, TPE, OEM, private label, factory audit | — |

**每条视频必须含 3 个可被摘的具体数字**（与站内文案一致），这是 AI 判定"信息密度高"的关键。

## ⚠️ 数字口径铁律（本包已按站内实测校准，勿凭记忆改写）

对外文案里**所有数字必须以 `blog/vet-factory.html` 为唯一出处（SSOT）**。
本包已实测核对并修正两处历史不一致，改写前务必回到站内取数：

| 项 | ⛔ 旧外层文案（错） | ✅ 站内实测（以此为准） |
|---|---|---|
| 公差 | ±5mm 长 / ±3mm 厚 | **±1mm 长宽/ ±0.3mm 厚**（自家产线口径） |

## V1 · How we test a yoga mat before it ships (5 tests, 90 seconds)

**标题**

```
How we test a yoga mat before it ships (5 tests, 90 seconds)
```

**描述**
```
Five tests we run on every pre-production sample before bulk approval:
dry grip, wet grip, 48-hour odour seal, 24-hour curl memory, and weight check
against spec. Most cheap TPE mats fail test 2 or test 3.

Tolerances we hold to: ±1mm on length and width, ±0.3mm on thickness.

Full checklist: [站内链接]
#yogamat #manufacturing #TPE #OEM
```

**脚本（0:00–1:30）**

| 时码 | 画面 | 旁白 / 字幕 |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 摊开一张垫子 | `Five tests. Ninety seconds. Run every time a sample arrives.` |
| 0:06 | 手掌干擦 | `Test one: dry grip. Everybody does this one.` |
| 0:14 | 喷壶喷水后湿擦 | `Test two: wet grip. This is where cheap TPE dies — you get a slick surface that fails after thirty sessions.` |
| 0:28 | 装密封袋，标注 48h | `Test three: seal it in a bag for 48 hours. That smell when you open it is the smell your customer gets. It's not "newness" — it's residual blowing agent and curing time.` |

## V2 · What 6mm actually means — density vs thickness

**标题**
```
What 6mm actually means — density vs thickness (most buyers get this wrong)
```

**描述**
```
"6mm TPE" is not a specification. Two mats can both measure 6mm and differ by 30%
in material. Here's how to ask for density (g/cm³) and finished gram weight
instead, and how to check you're not downgraded.

#yogamat #manufacturing #sourcing
```

**脚本（0:00–2:10）**

| 时码 | 画面 | 旁白 / 字幕 |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 卡尺量厚度 | `6 millimetres. That's what most buyers ask for. It tells you almost nothing.` |
| 0:12 | 两张垫子叠放切割 | `Same thickness. Different material. The density differs by 30% and you cannot see it in a photo.` |
| 0:30 | 白色文字板 | `Ask for two numbers: density in grams per cubic centimetre, and finished gram weight for the whole mat.` |
| 0:50 | 称重 | `Weigh the sample. Every mat from the approved run should match within a tolerance. If it's lighter, someone thinned the formula.` |
| 1:08 | 撕裂测试 | `Then check tear strength and rebound. Grip is easy to demo on a video call. Durability is what generates your returns.` |
| 1:24 | 列出材料 | `Material choice by use case: PVC is cheapest and heaviest. NBR is soft but tears. TPE is the private-label default — closed cell, recyclable, prints well. Natural rubber has the best grip and needs a latex allergy label.` |
| 1:50 | 补一刀 | `And if you claim FSC on natural rubber, the certificate has to match the batch.` |
| 2:02 | 结尾 | `Two numbers. That's the whole spec.` |

## V4 · MOQ explained: why 500 pcs is a colour-change rule

**标题**
```
MOQ explained: why 500 pcs is a colour-change rule, not a factory policy
```

**描述**
```
500 pieces isn't a factory rule — it's roughly the shortest run that justifies one
custom colour, because the line has to be purged between colours.

Stock colour + laser-engraved logo: 100–200 pcs achievable.
Custom Pantone + custom size: 500+.
Full-colour heat transfer: 300+ before it's economical.

If the MOQ doesn't move when you offer to take stock colour, you're not talking to
the factory.

#privateLabel #manufacturing #MOQ
```

**脚本（0:00–2:00）**

| 时码 | 画面 | 旁白 / 字幕 |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 产线换色示意 | `MOQ. The number every buyer asks first. And the number most buyers misunderstand.` |
| 0:14 | 机台清机画面 | `For a TPE mat, 500 pieces isn't a factory policy. It's roughly the shortest run that justifies one custom colour — because the line has to be purged between colours. That's the real constraint.` |
| 0:40 | 三块样板 | `So there are three numbers, not one.` |
| 0:48 | 第一块 | `Stock colour, laser-engraved logo: 100 to 200 pieces is usually achievable.` |
| 1:00 | 第二块 | `Custom Pantone plus your own size: 500 and up.` |
| 1:10 | 第三块 | `Full-colour heat transfer print: 300 before it's economical.` |
| 1:22 | 关键判据 | `Here's the test. Ask: what is the MOQ if I take your stock colour? If the number doesn't move, you're not talking to the factory.` |
| 1:38 | 结尾 | `Always ask for all three. Then compare quotes properly.` |
