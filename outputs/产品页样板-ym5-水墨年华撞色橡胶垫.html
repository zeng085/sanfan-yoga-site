Care & Maintenance

What your customers must know about a PU topPU 面垫子必须转告客户的事

Almost no competitor publishes this — and it is exactly what prevents 1-star reviews. Put it on your insert card.几乎没有同行会把这段写出来，而这恰恰是避免差评的关键。建议直接印成售后卡随货发出。

1 · Keep oil away1 · 怕油

The PU surface is porous — that is what makes it quick-drying, and also why it stains easily. Body oil included. Hands and feet clean before use.PU 表层有气孔，速干的同时极易沾污，尤其油渍（含人体油脂）。使用前请确保手脚洁净无油。

2 · How to clean2 · 清洁方式

Never use tea-tree or any oil-based detergent. A basin of water with one drop of dish soap; wipe with a well-wrung cloth; lay flat to dry. Do not soak.切勿使用含茶树或任何油性成分的清洁剂。一盆清水加一滴洗洁精，抹布沾湿挤干后擦拭，平铺晾干，切勿浸泡。

3 · Dry in shade3 · 通风阴干

After use, lay flat in a ventilated, shaded spot to air-dry. Natural rubber — never leave it in the sun.使用后置于通风背阴处平铺自然风干。含天然橡胶成分，切勿暴晒。

4 · Roll it correctly4 · 正确收纳

PU side (logo side) facing out; smooth from the wide edge; keep the edges even so no creases form.将带 LOGO 的 PU 面朝外，双手从宽边处抚平卷起，边缘收拢整齐防止褶皱。

FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask采购常问

Is every mat really a different pattern?每条垫子的纹路真的都不一样？

Yes — the marbling comes out of the colour-block process itself, so no two pieces match. This is a selling point, but state it in your listing and your customer-service script, otherwise it reads as a defect.是的——撞色水墨纹由工艺自然形成，没有两条相同。这是卖点，但必须在详情与客服话术里提前说明，否则会被当成瑕疵。

Why does a PU mat need care instructions?PU 垫为什么需要专门的保养说明？

The open-cell PU top that makes it dry fast also absorbs oil — including skin oil. Tea-tree cleaners and soaking both damage it. See the four care rules above.PU 表层的开孔结构带来速干性，同时也吸油（含人体油脂）。含茶树成分清洁剂与浸泡都会损伤垫面。参见上方四条保养规则。

183 cm or 185 cm — which one do I get?183cm 还是 185cm，我拿到的是哪个？

The colour-block SKUs are captioned 183 × 68 cm; the solid-colour family set is captioned 185 × 68 cm. Both lines run — tell us your target length and we quote against it. Tolerance ±3 cm, hand-measured.撞色款角标为 183×68cm，纯色家族图角标为 185×68cm。两条产线都在开，你按目标长度指定即可报价。手工测量公差 ±3cm。

How heavy is it, and does that matter for shipping?3300g 的重量对运费有影响吗？

3 kg per piece. Heavier than a TPE mat, so freight per unit is higher — ask us for EXW, FOB and DDP side by side and compare landed cost, not unit price.单条 3kg。比 TPE 垫重，单件运费更高——建议同时要 EXW / FOB / DDP 三种报价，比到岸成本而不是比单价。
