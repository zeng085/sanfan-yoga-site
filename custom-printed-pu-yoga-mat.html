Personal & private customization

Custom-printed PU yoga mats from 1 piece

Put your own photo, logo, name or artwork on a PU rubber yoga mat, starting from a single piece. No bulk minimum for this option: one mat, printed for one person, one gift or one design test.

What it is

This is personal, private customization on our PU rubber yoga mat: a PU top layer on a natural rubber base, printed with the image you send. It can be your photo, your studio or brand logo, a name, or a piece of artwork. You can order one mat, or a handful of different designs, without committing to a production run.

Why the grip stays

A printed surface on some mats ends up feeling slick. Our factory says its printing process keeps the PU surface anti-slip, so the mat keeps its grip after printing. The print goes onto the mat; the PU grip surface stays a PU grip surface.

How it is made: 3 steps

  1. Send your artwork. A photo, logo, name or design file. We check it with you before anything is printed.
  2. The mat goes on the flatbed printer. The PU rubber mat is laid flat on our UV flatbed printer.
  3. Printed. Your design is printed onto the mat surface.

Who it is for

  • Yoga and Pilates studios gifting members a mat with the studio logo or the member's name.
  • Personalized gifts: a mat with a photo, a name or a favourite artwork for one person.
  • Small brands testing designs before committing to a bulk order.
  • Online personalization sellers (Amazon or Etsy-style shops) who sell made-to-order printed mats.

Bulk OEM orders: MOQ still 300 pcs

The 1-piece option is for custom-printed PU rubber mats. Bulk OEM and private-label PU / rubber yoga mats keep the 300 pcs minimum in our MOQ policy. See the full PU & rubber yoga mat range.

FAQ

Can I really order just one custom-printed PU rubber yoga mat?

Yes. Custom-printed PU rubber yoga mats can be ordered from 1 piece. This is personal, private customization: one mat with your own photo, logo, name or artwork.

Does the print make the mat slippery?

According to our factory, no. The printing process we use keeps the PU surface anti-slip, so the mat keeps its grip after printing.

What can be printed on the mat?

Your photo, logo, name or artwork. Send us the file and we confirm it with you before printing.

What is the MOQ for bulk OEM PU rubber yoga mats?

Bulk OEM and private-label PU / rubber yoga mats are still 300 pcs, as set out in our MOQ policy. The 1-piece option applies to custom-printed PU rubber mats.
