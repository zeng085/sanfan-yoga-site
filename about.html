About SANFAN

Founded in 2013 and headquartered in Xiamen, Fujian, SANFAN is a leading OEM/ODM manufacturer of yoga mats and sports products, with our manufacturing base in Jinjiang — the heart of China’s sporting-goods belt.

We provide customized designing, developing, manufacturing and exporting for customers worldwide. Our range covers yoga mats, aerial yoga hammocks, yoga wheels, foam rollers, blocks, balls, resistance bands and a broad selection of fitness gear.

With 600+ skilled workers and high-tech machines, we guarantee high-output, high-quality production and on-time delivery at competitive prices. Our service team is on standby 24 hours to support our customers.

Production Lines

In-house from foaming to finishing. We operate three owned plants — two dedicated to TPE yoga mats and one to PU / rubber mats — running 6 foaming machines and 12 production lines. Because foaming, the most capacity-critical step, happens inside our own factories, we hold a steady output of 7,000–8,000 mats per day (about 200,000 per month) and control quality from raw material through to the finished mat.
