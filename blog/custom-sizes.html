Custom Yoga Mat Sizes & Thickness: A Buyer's Guide

Standard is not always what your market wants. This guide covers what is genuinely customizable on a yoga mat, what each change costs in tooling and minimums, and the few requests that no factory should say yes to.

Nearly everything visible on a yoga mat is customizable at FUJIAN SANFAN: length from 173 to 200 cm (68"–79"), width at 61, 80 or 122 cm (24" / 31" / 48"), thickness from 3 to 15 mm (1/8"–5/8"), Pantone-matched colours, two-tone double-layer builds, and logo by print, emboss, deboss or laser engraving. Cutting tolerance holds within ±1 mm on length and width and ±0.3 mm on thickness, and tooling for new sizes is made in-house, so a non-standard dimension rarely adds more than a few days to sampling. Three limits are honest ones: below 3 mm, print adhesion and cushioning both suffer; above 122 cm width, you need bespoke line setup; and photorealistic full-bleed print belongs on PU, not TPE. Send a one-page spec and you get a feasibility answer, a quote and a physical sample within 7 days.

Standard sizes, and why they are cheaper

The common format is 1830 × 610 mm (24"), with TPE thickness from 4 to 8 mm (5/32"–5/16") and up to 12 mm (1/2") for comfort lines. Standard sizes price lowest because the cutting patterns already exist and the line runs them without re-setup. Custom is not a premium product tier here; it is the same line with a different pattern, which is why the surcharge is usually smaller than buyers expect.

Wide formats are one of the simplest ways to stand out in a catalogue, because most competitors only stock 61 cm (24"). We also produce lengths up to about 200 cm (79"); beyond that the constraint is the foaming and curing equipment, not the cutting, so ask before you design around an extreme format.

Thickness options

  • 4–6 mm (5/32"–1/4"): portable, close-to-floor feel
  • 6–8 mm (1/4"–5/16"): balanced cushion and stability
  • 8–12 mm (5/16"–1/2"): extra joint comfort for sensitive knees
  • 3 mm (1/8"): precision spec for travel mats, produced to order

Each thickness is its own production run, because foaming and curing parameters differ. Mixing thicknesses inside one order is possible at higher total volumes; plan each thickness as a separate line item when you request the quote.

Tolerances and tooling

Cutting tolerance holds within ±1 mm on length and width, ±0.3 mm on thickness. Tooling for a new width or an embossing plate is produced in-house, which is why a new size rarely adds more than a few days to sampling. Before anything is cut, we send a spec confirmation drawing so the physical result matches your artwork exactly. This step has saved more launches than any other: a drawing costs nothing to change, a 3,000-pcs batch does not.

Colour matching: where custom jobs go wrong

Colour is the most common custom failure, and it fails quietly: the mat looks right in the showroom photo and wrong on the shelf. Our workflow is deliberately unexciting. Start from your Pantone or a physical reference. Produce a small colour chip on the actual material — TPE tints differently than PU, so the chip is always on the real substrate, never a screen mock-up. You confirm the chip, we lock it against the golden sample, and the batch is checked against that reference at every QC station. One confirmation step, and an entire off-colour batch never happens.

Logo, print and branding

Custom Pantone colours, two-tone double-layer constructions, and logo by printing, embossing or laser engraving, on the mat surface and on packaging. For the trade-offs between logo methods — durability, cost, which surface each one suits — see the methods table in our private label launch guide.

Packaging and extras

Custom boxes, belly bands, barcodes, QR codes and inserts, so the product arrives shelf-ready under your brand rather than in a polybag with a sticker. Decide packaging before bulk starts; late packaging decisions cost more and protect less.

What customization cannot do

A few limits are worth knowing up front. Extreme sub-3 mm thickness loses cushioning and print adhesion — we produce 1830 × 610 × 3 mm (1/8") as a precision travel spec, but it is a specialist build, not a general-purpose mat. Widths beyond 122 cm need a bespoke line setup with a matching minimum. Photorealistic full-bleed print works far better on PU than on TPE foam. Within those bounds, almost any private-label look is achievable. When a request pushes past what survives daily use, we will say so plainly on day one instead of shipping a problem.

From spec sheet to sample

Send a one-page spec — material, size, colour, logo, packaging — and you get a quote and a physical sample, typically within 7 days. The sample you approve becomes the bulk reference, so what you feel in your hand is what your customer receives. That continuity is the whole point of custom work: no surprises between the sample and the shipment.

FAQ

What is the largest yoga mat you can make?

Double-wide mats around 122 cm (48") are routine, and lengths up to about 200 cm (79") are workable in standard production. Beyond that the limit is usually the foaming and curing equipment rather than the cutting. If you need an extreme format, ask the factory to confirm against its widest line before you design around it.

Do custom sizes need new tooling?

Usually no new tooling is needed for a different length or width in the same material and thickness — it is a cutting-pattern change. New tooling becomes relevant for unusual thicknesses, special edge profiles or embossed patterns. Ask which category your request falls into, because the answer drives both cost and MOQ.

Can thickness vary within one order?

Yes, but treat each thickness as its own production run, because foaming and curing parameters differ. Mixing thicknesses inside one order is normally accepted only at higher total volumes. Plan each thickness as a separate line item when you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for a custom size?

Higher than a standard size, because the line must be set up for a non-standard cutting pattern. FUJIAN SANFAN produces non-standard specifications including 1830 x 610 x 3 mm (1/8"); confirm the exact MOQ alongside the price when you request a quote.

瑜伽垫定制尺寸与厚度：采购指南

标准款不一定是你的市场要的。这篇讲清瑜伽垫上哪些东西真正可定制、每项改动在模具和起订量上的代价，以及哪些要求工厂不该接。

在三梵，瑜伽垫上目之所及的东西几乎都可以定制：长度 173–200 cm（68"–79"），宽度 61／80／122 cm（24"／31"／48"），厚度 3–15 mm（1/8"–5/8"），Pantone 配色，双色双层结构，Logo 可印刷、压印、凹印或激光雕刻。裁切公差：长宽控制在 ±1 mm，厚度 ±0.3 mm；新尺寸的模具自家做，所以非标尺寸通常只给打样多添几天。三条边界是实话：低于 3 mm，印刷附着力和缓冲同时变差；宽度超过 122 cm 需要专门排线；照片级满版印花属于 PU，不属于 TPE。发来一页规格书，7 天内给你可行性答复、报价和实物样品。

标准尺寸，以及它为什么便宜

通用规格是 1830 × 610 mm（24"），TPE 厚度 4–8 mm（5/32"–5/16"），舒适线可做到 12 mm（1/2"）。标准尺寸单价最低，因为裁切版型现成、产线不用重新调试。定制在这里不是一个溢价产品层级，而是同一条线换一套版型——所以加价通常比采购预期的要小。

宽度：标准、加宽、双人宽

宽度适合谁商业提示
61 cm（24"）多数单人练习所有竞品都备的默认款
80 cm（31"）流瑜伽、肩宽较大的人产品线差异化的捷径；少有品牌在做
122 cm（48"）双人／家庭练习、场馆租赁健康品牌的强势占位

加宽规格是目录里最容易做出差异的做法之一，因为多数竞品只备 61 cm（24"）。长度我们也能做到约 200 cm（79"）；再往上，限制在发泡与熟化设备，不在裁切，所以设计极端规格之前先问一句。

厚度选项

  • 4–6 mm（5/32"–1/4"）：便携，贴地感强
  • 6–8 mm（1/4"–5/16"）：缓震与稳定兼顾
  • 8–12 mm（5/16"–1/2"）：关节更舒服，适合膝盖敏感的人
  • 3 mm（1/8"）：差旅垫的精密规格，按单生产

每种厚度都是独立的一次生产，因为发泡和熟化参数不同。总数量较高时，一张订单里混厚度是可行的；询报价时请把每种厚度列成单独一项。

公差与模具

裁切公差长宽控制在 ±1 mm，厚度 ±0.3 mm。新宽度或压印版的模具自家生产，所以新尺寸通常只给打样多添几天。开裁之前，我们先发规格确认图，让实物与图稿严格对上。这一步救过的上市比任何环节都多：改一张图不花钱，改 3,000 片的大货不便宜。

配色：定制翻车最常见的地方

颜色是最常见的定制失败点，而且它失败得很安静：展厅图看着对，上架看着不对。我们的流程故意做得很平淡。从你的 Pantone 号或实物参考起步；在真实材料上打一小块色片——TPE 和 PU 显色不同，所以色片永远打在真实基材上，绝不用屏幕上模拟；你确认色片，我们把它锁进黄金样，此后每一道 QC 都对着这个参考查。就一步确认，整批色偏不会发生。

Logo、印刷与品牌

定制 Pantone 颜色、双色双层结构，以及印刷、压印或激光雕刻的 Logo，可做在垫面，也可做在包装上。各种 Logo 工艺的取舍——耐用性、成本、各自适合什么表面——见我们自有品牌上市指南里的工艺对比表。

包装与配件

定制纸盒、腰封、条码、二维码和内插卡，让产品以你的品牌形态直接上架，而不是装在一个贴了标签的 PE 袋里。包装要在开大货之前定下来；定得晚，成本更高、保护更少。

定制做不到什么

有几条边界值得先知道。低于 3 mm 的极端厚度会同时损失缓冲和印刷附着力——1830 × 610 × 3 mm（1/8"）我们按精密差旅规格生产，但它是专门规格，不是通用垫。宽度超过 122 cm 需要专门排线，起订量也随之匹配。照片级满版印花在 PU 上远好过 TPE 发泡。在这些边界之内，几乎任何自有品牌外观都能实现。如果某个要求已经超出一张垫子日常使用的承受范围，我们会在第一天直说，而不是发一批问题出去。

从规格书到样品

发一页规格——材料、尺寸、颜色、Logo、包装——你会在 7 天内拿到报价和实物样品，通常就是这个节奏。你确认的样品就是大货的参照，所以手感上是什么样，客户收到的就是什么样。这种连续性正是定制的全部意义：样品和大货之间不出意外。

常见问题

你们能做多大尺寸？

122 cm（48"）左右的双人宽垫是常规活，长度做到约 200 cm（79"）在标准产线上可行。再往上，限制通常在发泡与熟化设备而不是裁切。如果你需要极端规格，先让工厂对着自家最宽的产线确认，再围绕它做设计。

定制尺寸需要开新模具吗？

同一材料、同一厚度下换长度或宽度，通常不需要新模具——那只是裁切版型的改动。新模具主要出现在特殊厚度、异形边或压花图案上。问清你的需求属于哪一类，因为答案同时决定成本和起订量。

一张订单里厚度可以不同吗？

可以，但要把每种厚度当作独立的一次生产，因为发泡和熟化参数不同。一张订单里混厚度，通常只在总数量较高时才接受。询报价时请把每种厚度列成单独一项。

定制尺寸的起订量是多少？

高于标准尺寸，因为产线要为非标裁切版型重新调试。三梵生产包括 1830 × 610 × 3 mm（1/8"）在内的非标规格；询价时请把确切的起订量和价格一起确认。
