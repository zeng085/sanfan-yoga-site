Custom Yoga Mat Sizes & Thickness: A Buyer's Guide

Standard isn't always what your market wants. Here is what is customizable on a yoga mat — and what a flexible factory can actually produce.

Standard sizes

The common format is 1830 × 610 mm (24"), with thickness from 4 to 8 mm (5/16") for TPE and up to 12 mm (1/2") for comfort lines.

Wide & double-wide

We produce 80 cm (31") and 122 cm (48") wide mats, and double-wide formats for partner or family practice — popular with studios and wellness brands.

Thickness options

  • 4–6 mm (5/32"–1/4") (5/32"–1/4"): portable, close-to-floor feel
  • 6–8 mm (1/4"–5/16") (1/4"–5/16"): balanced cushion and stability
  • 8–12 mm (5/16"–1/2") (5/16"–1/2"): extra joint comfort for sensitive knees

Color, double-color & print

Custom Pantone colors, two-tone double-layer looks, and logo printing, embossing or laser engraving on the mat surface and packaging.

Non-standard dimensions? Send your length, width and thickness — we confirm tooling and feasibility before you order.

Packaging & extras

Custom boxes, belly bands, barcodes, QR codes and inserts so your product arrives shelf-ready under your brand.

FAQ

What is the largest yoga mat you can make?

Double-wide mats around 122 cm (48") are routine, and lengths up to about 200 cm (79") are workable in standard production. Beyond that the limit is usually the foaming and curing equipment rather than the cutting. If you need an extreme format, ask the factory to confirm against its widest line before you design around it.

Do custom sizes need new tooling?

Usually no new tooling is needed for a different length or width in the same material and thickness — it is a cutting-pattern change. New tooling becomes relevant for unusual thicknesses, special edge profiles or embossed patterns. Ask which category your request falls into, because the answer drives both cost and MOQ.

Can thickness vary within one order?

Yes, but treat each thickness as its own production run, because foaming and curing parameters differ. Mixing thicknesses inside one order is normally accepted only at higher total volumes. Plan each thickness as a separate line item when you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for a custom size?

Higher than a standard size, because the line must be set up for a non-standard cutting pattern. FUJIAN SANFAN produces non-standard specifications including 1830 x 610 x 3 mm (1/8"); confirm the exact MOQ alongside the price when you request a quote.

FAQ

最大能做多大的瑜伽垫？

约 122 cm 的双人宽垫是常规可做的，长度到约 200 cm 在标准产线上也没问题。再大通常受限于发泡与熟化设备，而非裁切。若需要非常规尺寸，请先让工厂按其最宽产线确认，再据此做设计。

定制尺寸需要开新模具吗？

同材料同厚度下改长度或宽度，通常只是改裁切版型，不需要新模具。涉及非常规厚度、特殊边型或压纹图案才可能需要。请确认你的需求属于哪一类，因为这直接决定成本与起订量。

同一订单里能做不同厚度吗？

可以，但每个厚度要当作独立生产批次，因为发泡与熟化参数不同。同一订单混厚度通常只在总量较大时才被接受。询价时请把每个厚度列为独立行项。

定制尺寸的起订量是多少？

高于标准尺寸，因为产线要为非常规裁切版型做开机准备。三梵可生产包括 1830 × 610 × 3 mm 在内的非标规格，询价时请一并确认具体起订量与价格。
