Custom Yoga Mat Sizes & Thickness: A Buyer's Guide

Standard isn't always what your market wants. Here is what is customizable on a yoga mat — and what a flexible factory can actually produce.

Standard sizes

The common format is 1830 × 610 mm (24"), with thickness from 4 to 8 mm (5/16") for TPE and up to 12 mm (1/2") for comfort lines.

Wide & double-wide

We produce 80 cm (31") and 122 cm (48") wide mats, and double-wide formats for partner or family practice — popular with studios and wellness brands.

Thickness options

  • 4–6 mm (5/32"–1/4") (5/32"–1/4"): portable, close-to-floor feel
  • 6–8 mm (1/4"–5/16") (1/4"–5/16"): balanced cushion and stability
  • 8–12 mm (5/16"–1/2") (5/16"–1/2"): extra joint comfort for sensitive knees

Color, double-color & print

Custom Pantone colors, two-tone double-layer looks, and logo printing, embossing or laser engraving on the mat surface and packaging.

Non-standard dimensions? Send your length, width and thickness — we confirm tooling and feasibility before you order.

Packaging & extras

Custom boxes, belly bands, barcodes, QR codes and inserts so your product arrives shelf-ready under your brand.

Share your spec sheet and we will quote a fully customized mat — from material to shelf.

Custom specs we ship every week

  • Length 173–183 cm (68"–72"), width 61 / 80 / 122 cm (24" / 31" / 48")
  • Thickness 4–12 mm (5/32"–1/2") in TPE; thinner precision specs for the Japan/Korea market
  • Two-tone double-layer, embossed logo, laser engraving, single or dual-sided print
  • Custom Pantone colour matched to your brand book

Tolerances and tooling

Cutting tolerance holds within ±1 mm on length and width, ±0.3 mm on thickness. Tooling for a new width or embossing plate is produced in-house, which is why a new size rarely adds more than a few days to sampling. We send a spec confirmation drawing before cutting so the physical result matches your artwork exactly.

What customization cannot do

A few limits are worth knowing up front: extreme sub-3 mm thickness loses cushioning and print adhesion; widths beyond 122 cm need a bespoke line; and photorealistic full-bleed print is better on PU than TPE. Within those bounds, almost any private-label look is achievable. We will tell you plainly when a request pushes past what holds up in daily use.

From spec sheet to sample

Send a one-page spec — material, size, colour, logo, packaging — and we return a quote and a physical sample, typically within 7 days. The sample you approve is the reference for bulk, so what you feel in your hand is what your customer receives. No surprises between sample and shipment.

Colour-matching workflow

Colour is where custom jobs go wrong most often. We start from your Pantone or physical reference, produce a small colour chip on the actual material, and confirm with you before the full run. TPE tints slightly differently than PU, so the chip is always on the real substrate — never a screen mock-up. That one confirmation step prevents an entire off-colour batch.

Key takeaway

Customization is not a premium add-on here — it is the default way we work. Send a one-page spec and you get a matched sample and a firm quote, with the sample you approve becoming the bulk standard. The fastest path from idea to shelf-ready product is a clear spec and a factory that owns its tooling.

FAQ

What is the largest yoga mat you can make?

Double-wide mats around 122 cm (48") are routine, and lengths up to about 200 cm (79") are workable in standard production. Beyond that the limit is usually the foaming and curing equipment rather than the cutting. If you need an extreme format, ask the factory to confirm against its widest line before you design around it.

Do custom sizes need new tooling?

Usually no new tooling is needed for a different length or width in the same material and thickness — it is a cutting-pattern change. New tooling becomes relevant for unusual thicknesses, special edge profiles or embossed patterns. Ask which category your request falls into, because the answer drives both cost and MOQ.

Can thickness vary within one order?

Yes, but treat each thickness as its own production run, because foaming and curing parameters differ. Mixing thicknesses inside one order is normally accepted only at higher total volumes. Plan each thickness as a separate line item when you request a quote.

What is the MOQ for a custom size?

Higher than a standard size, because the line must be set up for a non-standard cutting pattern. FUJIAN SANFAN produces non-standard specifications including 1830 x 610 x 3 mm (1/8"); confirm the exact MOQ alongside the price when you request a quote.

瑜伽垫尺寸与厚度定制：采购者指南

标准款未必是你的市场想要的。这里讲清瑜伽垫哪些能定制——以及柔性工厂实际能做多少。

标准尺寸

常见规格为 1830 × 610 毫米（约 72 × 24 英寸），厚度 TPE 为 4–8 毫米，舒适线可达 12 毫米。

加宽与双倍宽

我们生产 80 厘米与 122 厘米宽垫，以及双人/亲子练习用的双倍宽款式——在瑜伽馆与健康品牌中很受欢迎。

厚度选项

  • 4–6 毫米：便携、贴近地面感
  • 6–8 毫米：缓冲与稳定平衡
  • 8–12 毫米：对膝盖敏感者更友好的额外关节保护

颜色、双色与印花

可定制 Pantone 专色、双色双层外观，以及在垫面与包装上做 logo 印刷、压印或激光雕刻。

非标尺寸？把长、宽、厚发来——我们在你下单前确认模具与可行性。

包装与配件

定制彩盒、腰封、条码、二维码与插页，让你的产品以自有品牌、可上架状态送达。

把规格表发来，我们为你报一款从材质到货架全定制的瑜伽垫。

我们每周都在出的定制规格

  • 长度 173–183 cm（68"–72"），宽度 61 / 80 / 122 cm（24" / 31" / 48"）
  • 厚度 TPE 4–12 mm（5/32"–1/2"）；日本韩国市场可做更薄精密规格
  • 双色双层、压印 logo、激光雕刻、单面或双面印刷
  • 按你的品牌色卡定制潘通色

公差与模具

裁切公差长宽控制在 ±1 mm、厚度 ±0.3 mm。新宽度或压印板的模具在厂内制作，所以新尺寸通常只给打样多加几天。裁切前我们发确认图，让实物与你的图稿完全一致。

定制做不到的边界

有几条值得先知道：低于 3 mm 的超薄会损失缓冲与印刷附着力；超过 122 cm 的宽度需要专用线；照片级满版印刷在 PU 上比 TPE 更稳。在此边界内，几乎任何自有品牌外观都能实现。当某个需求撑不住日常使用，我们会直说。

从规格表到样

发一页规格——材料、尺寸、颜色、logo、包装——我们回报价并出实物样，通常 7 天内。你确认的样就是大货基准，所以到你手里的触感就是客户收到的。样与货之间不 surprises。

配色工作流程

颜色是定制最容易翻车的地方。我们从你的潘通或实物参考出发，在真实材料上先出小色片，全批前与你确认。TPE 与 PU 上色略有差异，所以色片永远做在真实基材上——绝不是屏幕模拟。这一步确认能挡掉整批偏色。

重点

定制在这里不是加价选项，而是我们的常态做法。发一页规格，你就拿到匹配样与实价，而你确认的样就是大货基准。从想法到上架库存最快的路，是一份清晰规格加一家自有模具的工厂。

FAQ

最大能做多大的瑜伽垫？

约 122 cm 的双人宽垫是常规可做的，长度到约 200 cm 在标准产线上也没问题。再大通常受限于发泡与熟化设备，而非裁切。若需要非常规尺寸，请先让工厂按其最宽产线确认，再据此做设计。

定制尺寸需要开新模具吗？

同材料同厚度下改长度或宽度，通常只是改裁切版型，不需要新模具。涉及非常规厚度、特殊边型或压纹图案才可能需要。请确认你的需求属于哪一类，因为这直接决定成本与起订量。

同一订单里能做不同厚度吗？

可以，但每个厚度要当作独立生产批次，因为发泡与熟化参数不同。同一订单混厚度通常只在总量较大时才被接受。询价时请把每个厚度列为独立行项。

定制尺寸的起订量是多少？

高于标准尺寸，因为产线要为非常规裁切版型做开机准备。三梵可生产包括 1830 × 610 × 3 mm 在内的非标规格，询价时请一并确认具体起订量与价格。
