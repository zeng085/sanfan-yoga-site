Yoga Mat MOQ Explained: How Low Can You Go

MOQ decides how much cash you tie up before you know if a product sells. Here is what "low MOQ" really means at a factory — and how to use it.

Our MOQ by product

  • TPE yoga mats: 100 pcs per design and color
  • Foam rollers / EVA blocks: 500 pcs
  • Most accessories: 500–1,000 pcs

Why low MOQ matters

A 100-pcs start lets a new brand validate demand without a warehouse of inventory. You can test one color and size, read the market, then reorder what actually sells.

How to use a low MOQ well

Start with a single hero color, confirm quality with a 7-day sample, list it, and only scale once orders come in. Low MOQ turns a bet into an experiment.

Low MOQ is not lower quality — it is better production scheduling. We hold material stock so small runs stay efficient.

Common MOQ myths

  • Lower MOQ ≠ lower quality. It reflects how we batch production.
  • "No MOQ" usually means a trader reselling stock, not a real factory.
  • MOQ is per design/color, so more variants raise the minimum.

FAQ

Why do factories set a minimum order quantity?

Every production run carries fixed setup costs — colour matching, heating the foaming line, calibrating cutting tools and cleaning between colours. Below a certain quantity those fixed costs cannot be spread across enough units, so the per-unit price stops making sense. MOQ is where that equation balances.

Can I mix colours within one MOQ?

Usually yes, up to a point. At FUJIAN SANFAN the TPE yoga mat MOQ is 100 pcs per design and colour, so a 300-piece order could cover three colours. Ask your supplier to state exactly how the minimum is counted — per design, per colour, or per order total.

Does a lower MOQ mean a higher unit price?

Almost always. Set-up costs are fixed, so spreading them over 100 pieces gives a higher per-unit figure than spreading them over 5,000. The trade-off is inventory risk: paying a little more per unit to test a market is often cheaper than sitting on unsold stock.

Can one MOQ be split across different sizes?

Sometimes, but each size change can mean a cutting-pattern adjustment. Splitting across sizes is usually accepted at higher total volumes. Confirm it in writing before you plan a mixed order.

瑜伽垫起订量详解：到底能做多低

起订量决定了你在确认产品好卖之前要压多少现金。这里讲清工厂里"低起订"真正意味着什么，以及如何用它。

各品类起订量

  • TPE 瑜伽垫：100 片/款/色
  • 瑜伽柱 / EVA 砖：500 个
  • 多数配件：500–1,000 件

低起订为何重要

100 片起步让新品牌在不动用大量库存的情况下验证需求。你可以先测一个颜色一个尺寸，看市场反馈，再补单真正好卖的款。

如何用好低起订

先做一个主推色，用 7 天样确认品质，上架后等订单进来再放大。低起订把"押注"变成"实验"。

低起订不等于低品质——而是更优的生产排程。我们常备原料，所以小批量也能高效。

关于起订量的常见误区

  • 更低起订 ≠ 更低品质，它反映的是我们的生产批次方式。
  • "无起订"通常是贸易商倒卖库存，而非真实工厂。
  • 起订量按款/色计算，变体越多，最低量越高。

FAQ

工厂为什么要设起订量？

每批生产都有固定开机成本——配色备料、发泡线升温、裁切刀具校准、换色清机。数量低于某个点，这些固定成本摊不到足够多的产品上，单价就失去意义。MOQ 就是这道算术的平衡点。

一个起订量内可以混色吗？

通常可以，但有限度。三梵 TPE 瑜伽垫是 100 片/款/色起，因此 300 片的订单可做三个颜色。下单前请让供应商写清最小量如何计算——按款、按色，还是按订单总量。

起订量越低单价越高吗？

基本是。开机成本固定，摊到 100 片与摊到 5,000 片，单片成本完全不同。代价是库存风险：为测试市场而多付一点单价，往往比压着卖不掉的库存更划算。

一个起订量能拆分不同尺寸吗？

有时可以，但每次换尺寸可能涉及裁切版型调整。通常总量较大时才接受拆尺寸。计划混尺寸订单前请书面确认。
