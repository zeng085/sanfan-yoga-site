7-Day Sampling: Why Fast Prototyping Wins Private-Label Brands

Speed is a competitive advantage most buyers underestimate. A one-week sample cycle changes how fast you can launch — and how much risk you carry.

What a 7-day sample covers

From your spec, we produce a physical sample with your logo, color and packaging options, then ship it for your review — typically within 7 days.

Why it matters

  • Iterate faster: see and feel the real product before committing a bulk run.
  • Beat competitors: a faster sample-to-shelf cycle wins seasonal and trend windows.
  • Lower risk: fix color, grip or packaging on the sample, not after 5,000 units arrive.
A week to a real, branded sample is the difference between guessing and knowing.

How we do it

In-house tooling, stock material formulations and a dedicated sampling team let us turn a spec into a physical sample in days, not weeks. Custom developments from your sketch or sample follow the same fast path.

Make the most of it

Send your target dimensions, preferred material and brand colors up front. The clearer the brief, the closer the first sample is to production-ready.

FAQ

Does a 7-day sample include custom packaging?

It can. Send your artwork with the sample request and the prototype can be packed in your own printed sleeve or box rather than neutral packaging. Packaging adds a little time when it needs printing, so confirm the artwork format early.

How many sample rounds are normal?

One to two rounds for most projects. The first sample confirms material, thickness, colour and logo placement. A second round appears when a specification needs adjusting. More than three rounds usually means the brief was unclear, not that the factory is slow.

Do I pay for samples?

Most factories charge for samples and credit the cost against your bulk order. The sample fee covers setup and material, and it filters out non-serious enquiries. Ask up front whether the fee is refundable on order.

What if the sample is not right?

Give specific, measurable feedback — thickness in millimetres, a colour reference, a grip expectation, logo position — rather than a vague feeling. A factory can act on a number; it cannot act on a feeling. That is the single biggest factor in how fast round two arrives.

7 天打样：为何快速出样能赢品牌商

速度是多数买家低估的竞争优势。一周出样周期，会直接改变你上市的速度，以及所承担的风险。

7 天出样包含什么

依据你的规格，我们生产带 logo、配色与包装选项的实物样品，并发出供你确认——通常 7 天内完成。

为何重要

  • 更快迭代：大货前先看、先摸真实产品。
  • 跑赢对手：更快的"样品到上架"周期，抢占季节与潮流窗口。
  • 更低风险：在样品上改颜色、抓地力或包装，而非等 5000 件到货才发现问题。
一周拿到带品牌的实物样，区别就在"猜测"与"确认"之间。

我们如何做到

如何用好它

FAQ

7 天样品包含定制包装吗？

可以。下样品单时一并提交设计稿，样品就能用你自己的印刷套袋或彩盒，而非中性包装。若包装需要印刷会多花一点时间，请尽早确认稿件格式。

样品通常要打几轮？

多数项目 1 到 2 轮。第一轮确认材料、厚度、颜色与 logo 位置；规格需要调整时才有第二轮。超过三轮通常说明需求描述不清，而不是工厂慢。

样品要收费吗？

多数工厂收样品费，并在大货订单中返还。样品费覆盖开机与材料成本，也能筛掉非诚意询价。下单前请确认费用是否可退。

样品不满意怎么办？

给可量化的具体反馈——厚度几毫米、颜色对照、防滑预期、logo 位置——而不是含糊的手感描述。工厂能执行数字，执行不了感觉。这是决定第二轮多快到达的最大因素。
