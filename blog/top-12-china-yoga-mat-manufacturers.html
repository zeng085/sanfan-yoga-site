Top 12 Yoga Mat Manufacturers in China (2026): Cluster-by-Cluster Comparison

Finding a yoga mat factory in China is easy — finding one that fits your MOQ, material and compliance requirements is not. This guide compares 12 established manufacturers across China's six yoga-mat clusters, compiled from each company's own public audit listings and official pages.

How this list was compiled: all figures below (MOQ, capacity, certifications) are self-reported by each manufacturer in public sources — Made-in-China / Alibaba audit pages, official company sites and third-party trade listings — collected in September 2026. They are a starting point for shortlisting, not a substitute for your own verification. Companies are grouped by industrial cluster, not ranked.

Jiangsu cluster (Yancheng / Zhangjiagang) — the foam & rubber belt

Jiangsu, north of Shanghai, is China's densest belt of foam and rubber mat plants. Expect the widest PVC/TPE material menus, competitive FOB pricing and factories accustomed to large repeat orders.

1. Jiangsu YDB Industrial Co., Ltd. — Yancheng

A frequently cited export-oriented manufacturer with a broad PVC, TPE and exercise-mat range and deep customization options for private-label programs. Best for: large retailers and distributors consolidating PVC and TPE volume with one supplier.

2. Zhangjiagang Huayi Rubber Co., Ltd. — Zhangjiagang

Polyethylene-foam specialist about two hours from Shanghai, producing PVC, TPE, NBR, EVA and PU foam. Publishes MOQ 500 pcs, ~50,000 pcs/month and EN71, 6P, REACH and RoHS certification support; 15+ years of production history. Best for: EU-bound private-label programs that need REACH documentation from day one.

3. Yancheng Tianzhihui Sports Goods (TIANHUI) — Yancheng

The sports-industry division of Jiangsu Crosste Group, with 10 production lines spanning yoga, judo and taekwondo mats plus rubber flooring. Reports roughly 500,000 yoga mats per year and US$50–100 million annual output; ISO 9001 and BSCI audited. Best for: buyers building mixed martial-arts-plus-yoga assortments.

4. Jiangsu Huayang Sports — Jiangsu

One of the larger dedicated mat plants: 25,000+ ㎡, an estimated 150,000–200,000 mats per month, MOQ 1,000 pcs, SGS-audited, exporting to 40+ countries across TPE, NBR, PVC and natural rubber. Best for: established brands scaling large, consistent orders with mature documentation (COA and compliance packs provided upfront).

Zhejiang cluster (Ningbo / Yiwu) — premium & accessory-rich

Zhejiang pairs two of China's biggest ports with a supplier base strong in R&D-led products and category breadth — convenient when mats ship together with blocks, straps and rollers.

5. Ningbo Healthmate Science and Technology — Ninghai, Ningbo

Founded 2007; around 100,000 ㎡, 850 staff and roughly 700,000 units per year across fitness equipment. In yoga mats the company differentiates through patented moisture-activated grip coatings and antibacterial surface treatments, positioning in the premium US$10–18 band. Best for: premium, tech-differentiated mat lines where grip performance is the selling point.

6. Ningbo Virson Sporting Goods — Ningbo

Founded 2008 on a 10,000 ㎡ campus with two PVC lines, a Taiwan-sourced NBR line and a TPE line; EN71-3 tested. Best for: EU/US distributors bundling mats with fitness accessories under one purchasing workflow.

7. Yiwu Yunhuan Trading — Yiwu

TPE anti-slip specialist producing 50,000–80,000 mats per month at MOQ 500, with closed-cell TPE that resists moisture absorption and FOB pricing around US$4.50–8. Best for: small-to-mid brands testing market entry with controlled initial risk.

Hubei cluster (Wuhan) — vertical-integration specialists

Wuhan's newer generation of factories competes on vertical supply chains (in-house foaming to packaging) and eco-certification density rather than port proximity.

8. Wuhan FDM Eco Fitness Product Co., Ltd. (FDM Yoga / Uga Sport)

Founded 2015 within Hubei Freedom Group; 30,000–40,000 ㎡ across facilities, producing TPE, PU, natural rubber, cork and suede mats with ISO 9001, BSCI, GRS, FSC, OEKO-TEX 100, REACH and RoHS on file. MOQ from 100 pcs; supplies European supermarket chains including Aldi and Rossmann; exports to 30+ countries. Best for: eco-certified lines and retail-chain programs where GRS/FSC paperwork is a gate, not a bonus.

9. Wuhan Ugasport Co., Ltd.

22,354 ㎡ of floor space, six production lines and 88 machines, with REACH and OEKO-TEX 100 certificates on file. Publishes TPE mat pricing around US$2.88–3.78 at MOQ 100 pcs; core markets are North America (~25%), Northern Europe and Eastern Europe. Best for: price-driven TPE programs that still need low MOQ and recognized eco-certificates.

Shandong cluster (Qingdao) — PVC foam heritage

10. Qingdao Kingking Applied Chemistry Co., Ltd.

A Qingdao manufacturer known for PVC foam and related mat products, with mass-production experience and private-label supply for international markets. Best for: budget PVC lines at scale where unit cost outweighs material novelty.

Guangdong cluster (Dongguan) — studio-grade builds

11. Dongguan GY Industrial Co., Ltd.

A yoga-focused factory offering natural rubber, PU and TPE configurations with higher-grip builds aimed at studio and premium retail positioning. Best for: yoga studios and premium DTC brands where grip feel matters more than unit cost.

Fujian cluster (Jinjiang / Quanzhou) — the sports-goods corridor

12. FUJIAN SANFAN Sports Products Co., Ltd.

Based in Jinjiang, Quanzhou — China's sportswear and sports-goods corridor with direct access to Xiamen port. Established 2013; 12,000 ㎡; 600+ staff; around 8,000 mats per day on a dual-color TPE dual-layer line, plus PU/rubber mats, foam rollers, resistance bands and fitness equipment. Publishes MOQ 100 pcs, 7-day sampling, ISO 9001 and BSCI audits, and ROHS, CE and REACH compliance. Best for: startups, Amazon sellers and boutique brands that need low MOQ, fast samples and one supplier covering the full yoga-plus-fitness catalog.

How to choose between them

  • Match material to market. TPE wins e-commerce economics (a 6 mm mat weighs ~1.1 kg vs ~2.4 kg for PU-rubber); rubber/PU wins hot-yoga grip. TPE vs PU compared →
  • Match MOQ to cashflow. 100-piece MOQs exist (FDM, Ugasport, SANFAN); 1,000+ piece MOQs buy better unit prices. How MOQ really works →
  • Match certifications to destination. EU needs REACH; US retail often wants CA Prop 65; supermarket chains add BSCI/GRS on top. Compliance by market →
  • Verify before contracting. Samples, in-house-vs-outsourced process questions, certificate numbers and a small pilot order — the full checklist in how to vet a factory →

在中国找瑜伽垫工厂不难，难的是找到起订量、材质、认证都合得上的那一家。这篇按六大产区对比 12 家成熟制造商，资料全部取自各家公开发布的审核信息与官网。

江苏产区（盐城／张家港）——发泡与橡胶主带

江苏位于上海以北，是中国发泡垫与橡胶垫工厂最密集的地带。PVC／TPE 材质菜单最全、FOB 价格竞争性强，工厂习惯接大批量返单。

1. Jiangsu YDB Industrial — 盐城

被多方引用的出口型厂商，PVC、TPE 及各类体操垫产品线很宽，自有品牌定制能力强。适合：想用一家供应商整合 PVC＋TPE 出货量的大型零售商与经销商。

2. Zhangjiagang Huayi Rubber — 张家港

距上海约两小时车程的发泡材料专业厂，产 PVC、TPE、NBR、EVA、PU 五类发泡品。公开起订量 500 片、月产能约 5 万片，支持 EN71、6P、REACH、RoHS，生产历史 15 年以上。适合：第一天就要 REACH 文件、主攻欧盟的自有品牌项目。

3. Yancheng Tianzhihui（TIANHUI）— 盐城

江苏 Crosste 集团旗下运动板块，10 条线覆盖瑜伽垫、柔道垫、跆拳道垫与橡胶地垫。自报瑜伽垫年产约 50 万片、年产值 5,000 万–1 亿美元，通过 ISO 9001 与 BSCI 审核。适合：需要"瑜伽＋格斗垫"混合配货的买家。

4. Jiangsu Huayang Sports — 江苏

规模较大的专业垫厂之一：厂房 25,000 ㎡ 以上、月产能估计 15 万–20 万片、起订量 1,000 片、通过 SGS 审核，TPE／NBR／PVC／天然橡胶四条线，出口 40 多个国家。适合：成熟品牌需要大批量稳定交付，且 COA 与合规包能提前给齐。

浙江产区（宁波／义乌）——高端与配件齐备

浙江同时坐拥中国两个大港，供应商在研发型产品与品类宽度上更强 —— 垫子要和瑜伽砖、伸展带、泡沫轴同柜出货时特别方便。

5. Ningbo Healthmate — 宁波宁海

2007 年成立，厂房约 10 万㎡、员工 850 人，健身器材年产量约 70 万台。瑜伽垫靠专利"湿手防滑"涂层与抗菌表层做差异，定价落在高端 10–18 美元区间。适合：以抓地性能为卖点的高端科技型产品线。

6. Ningbo Virson — 宁波

2008 年成立，园区 10,000 ㎡，两条 PVC 线 ＋ 一条台湾产 NBR 线 ＋ 一条 TPE 线，通过 EN71-3。适合：要在同一采购流程里把垫子和健身配件一起打包的欧美经销商。

7. Yiwu Yunhuan — 义乌

TPE 防滑专业厂，月产 5 万–8 万片、起订量 500 片，闭孔 TPE 不易吸潮，FOB 报价约 4.5–8 美元。适合：以小批量试水、控制初期风险的中小品牌。

湖北产区（武汉）——垂直一体化

武汉这一代工厂比的是垂直供应链（从自有发泡到包装）和环保认证密度，而不是港口距离。

8. Wuhan FDM Eco Fitness（FDM Yoga / Uga Sport）

2015 年成立于湖北自由集团（Hubei Freedom Group）体系内，多处厂区合计 3 万–4 万㎡，产 TPE、PU、天然橡胶、软木、麂皮垫，持有 ISO 9001、BSCI、GRS、FSC、OEKO-TEX 100、REACH、RoHS。起订量最低 100 片；据公开资料为欧洲超市渠道（含 Aldi、Rossmann）供货，出口 30 多个国家。适合：需要 GRS／FSC 这类环保资质作为准入门槛、而非加分项的商超项目。

9. Wuhan Ugasport

厂房 22,354 ㎡、6 条生产线、88 台设备，持有 REACH 与 OEKO-TEX 100。公开 TPE 垫报价约 2.88–3.78 美元（100 片起）；主要市场为北美（约 25%）、北欧与东欧。适合：价格优先、但仍需要低起订量与主流环保认证的 TPE 项目。

山东产区（青岛）——PVC 发泡老基地

10. Qingdao Kingking

青岛厂商，以 PVC 发泡及同类垫材见长，具备大批量生产经验，为国际市场做自主品牌供货。适合：单价权重高于材质新奇度的经济型 PVC 大货。

广东产区（东莞）——馆级高抓地

11. Dongguan GY Industrial

专注瑜伽的工厂，提供天然橡胶、PU 与 TPE 配置，走更高抓地的构建，面向瑜伽馆与高端零售定位。适合：抓地手感比单价更重要的瑜伽馆与高端 DTC 品牌。

福建产区（晋江／泉州）——运动用品走廊

12. 福建三梵 FUJIAN SANFAN Sports Products

位于晋江，正处中国运动服装与运动用品走廊，直连厦门港。2013 年成立；厂房 12,000 ㎡；员工 600+；双色 TPE 双层线日产约 8,000 片，另有 PU／橡胶垫、泡沫轴、弹力带与健身器材。公开起订量 100 片、7 天打样，通过 ISO 9001 与 BSCI 审核，具备 ROHS、CE、REACH 合规。适合：需要低起订量、快速打样，且想用一家供应商覆盖"瑜伽＋健身"全目录的初创品牌、亚马逊卖家与精品品牌。

怎么在这 12 家里做选择

  • 材质跟着市场走。 TPE 赢在电商经济性（6mm 垫约 1.1kg，PU 橡胶约 2.4kg）；橡胶／PU 赢在高温瑜伽的抓地。TPE 与 PU 对比 →
  • 起订量跟着现金流走。 100 片起的工厂确实存在（FDM、Ugasport、SANFAN）；1,000 片以上能换到更好的单价。起订量到底怎么算 →
  • 认证跟着目的地走。 欧盟要 REACH；美国零售常要 CA Prop 65；商超渠道再加 BSCI／GRS。分市场合规要求 →
  • 签约前先核验。 样品、自制还是外发工序、证书编号、小批量试单 —— 完整清单见如何验厂 →
