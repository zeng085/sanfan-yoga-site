Yoga Mat Thickness & Size Guide: How to Specify a Mat (2026)

Thickness, length, width and density are the four numbers that define a yoga mat — and the four most common sources of buyer's remorse. This guide maps each spec to the practice it actually suits, and flags where custom sizes cost more.

Thickness: match it to the practice, not to "more is better"

ThicknessBest forTrade-off
3 mm (1/8")Travel, balance work, advanced practiceLittle cushioning; folds or rolls very small
4–5 mm (5/32"–3/16") (5/32"–3/16")General purpose — the industry standardBalanced cushion and stability
6 mm (1/4")Beginners, sensitive knees and wristsSlightly less stable in standing balances
8–10 mm (5/16"–3/8") (5/16"–3/8")Pilates, floor work, rehabilitationBulky to carry; soft underfoot
12–15 mm (1/2"–5/8") (1/2"–5/8")Children's mats, physiotherapy, senior useHeavy; not suited to standing yoga

Above roughly 8 mm (5/16"), standing balances get measurably harder: the mat compresses under the foot and the ankle has to work to stabilise. If your customers do standing flows, do not oversell thickness.

Length: size against user height

The standard mat is 183 cm (72") long, which suits practitioners up to about 180 cm (71"). Anyone taller should specify 185–200 cm (73"–79") (73"–79") (73–79"). A reliable rule: mat length = user height + about 10 cm (4") of headroom, so both the head and heels stay on the surface in Savasana.

Width: standard, wide and double-wide

  • 61 cm (24") — standard width, right for most solo practice.
  • 80 cm (31") — wide format for dynamic flows and broader shoulder placement.
  • 122 cm (48") — double-wide, fits two people side by side; popular for partner yoga, family use and studio rental fleets.

Wide mats are a straightforward way to differentiate a product line, because most competitors only stock 61 cm (24").

Density: the spec most buyers forget

Density is how much material is packed into the mat. Two 6 mm (1/4") mats can feel completely different: a low-density one bottoms out under pressure, while a high-density one supports the joint and lasts longer. Higher density means more material, more weight and higher unit cost — so decide whether your line competes on feel or on landed cost.

Non-standard sizes

Standard sizes are cheaper because the tooling and cutting patterns already exist. We do produce non-standard specifications — including 1830 × 610 × 3 mm (1/8") and other custom length, width and thickness combinations — but custom dimensions require a higher minimum quantity. Ask for the MOQ alongside the price when you request a quote.

FAQ

What thickness yoga mat should I buy?

3 mm (1/8") for travel and balance-focused practice, 4–5 mm (5/32"–3/16") (5/32"–3/16") as the general-purpose standard, 6 mm (1/4") for beginners and anyone with sensitive knees, and 8–15 mm (5/16"–5/8") (5/16"–5/8") for Pilates, rehabilitation, children's mats or floor exercise. Thicker is not always better — above 8 mm (5/16"), standing balances get harder because the mat compresses underfoot.

How long should a yoga mat be?

A standard mat is 183 cm (72") long, which suits practitioners up to about 180 cm (71") tall. Taller users should specify 185–200 cm (73"–79") (73"–79") (73–79"). Always size the mat against the user's height plus roughly 10 cm (4") of headroom.

What is a wide yoga mat for?

Standard width is 61 cm (24"). A wide mat (around 80 cm (31")) gives room for dynamic flows and broader shoulder placement, and a double-wide mat (around 122 cm (48")) fits two people practising side by side. Wide formats are popular for partner yoga, family use and studio rental fleets.

Can I order non-standard yoga mat sizes?

Yes. FUJIAN SANFAN produces non-standard specifications including 1830 × 610 × 3 mm (1/8") and other custom length, width and thickness combinations. Custom sizes do require a larger minimum quantity than standard sizes, so confirm the MOQ when requesting a quote.

What does mat density mean?

Density describes how much material is packed into the mat. Higher density means better support and longer life but more weight. A low-density 8 mm (5/16") mat can feel softer yet bottom out under pressure, while a high-density 5 mm (3/16") mat may offer better joint protection.

瑜伽垫厚度与尺寸规格指南（2026）

厚度、长度、宽度、密度——这四个数字定义了一张瑜伽垫，也是买家最容易后悔的四个点。本指南把每个参数对应到真实使用场景，并说明定制尺寸的成本差异。

厚度：按场景选，不是越厚越好

厚度适用场景取舍
3 mm旅行、平衡体式、进阶练习缓冲少；可折叠、收纳体积小
4–5 mm通用——行业标准缓冲与稳定平衡
6 mm初学者、膝盖手腕敏感者站立平衡略逊
8–10 mm普拉提、地面动作、康复训练携带笨重；脚感偏软
12–15 mm儿童垫、理疗、长者使用很重；不适合站立体式

超过约 8 mm 后，站立平衡会明显变难：垫子在脚下压缩，脚踝要额外发力稳定。如果你的客户做站立体式串联，不要把厚度当成卖点硬推。

长度：按身高选

标准垫长 183 cm，适合身高约 180 cm 以下的使用者。更高的人应选 185–200 cm。可靠规则：垫长 = 身高 + 约 10 cm 余量，保证挺尸式时头与脚跟都在垫面上。

宽度：标准 / 加宽 / 双人

  • 61 cm —— 标准宽度，适合多数单人练习。
  • 80 cm —— 加宽，适合动态串联与更宽的肩部摆放。
  • 122 cm —— 双人宽，可两人并排；常见于双人瑜伽、家庭使用与瑜伽馆租赁。

加宽垫是做产品线差异化的捷径，因为多数竞品只备 61 cm 一个规格。

密度：最常被忽略的参数

密度指单位体积内的材料量。两张 6 mm 的垫子手感可能天差地别：低密度受压会塌陷，高密度能支撑关节且更耐用。高密度意味着更多材料、更重、单价更高——先想清楚你的产品线是靠脚感还是靠到手成本竞争。

常见问题

该买多厚的瑜伽垫？

3 mm 适合旅行与专注平衡的练习；4–5 mm 是通用标准；6 mm 适合初学者与膝盖敏感者；8–15 mm 适合普拉提、康复、儿童垫或地面训练。并非越厚越好——超过 8 mm，站立平衡会因垫子压缩而变难。

瑜伽垫该多长？

标准垫长 183 cm，适合身高约 180 cm 以下者。更高的人应选 185–200 cm。原则是垫长 = 身高 + 约 10 cm 余量。

加宽垫有什么用？

标准宽 61 cm。加宽垫（约 80 cm）给动态串联与更宽肩距留空间；双人垫（约 122 cm）可两人并排，适合双人瑜伽、家庭与瑜伽馆租赁。

能定制非标尺寸吗？

可以。三梵生产非标规格，包括 1830 × 610 × 3 mm 及其他长宽厚组合。定制尺寸起订量高于标准尺寸，询价时请确认 MOQ。

密度是什么意思？

密度指单位体积内的材料量。密度越高，支撑与寿命越好，但更重。低密度 8 mm 垫脚感软但受压易塌陷；高密度 5 mm 垫对关节的保护可能更好。
