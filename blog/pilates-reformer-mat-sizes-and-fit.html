Pilates Reformer Mat Sizes and Fit: How to Match a Mat to Your Carriage

Short answer: there is no standard Pilates reformer mat size. Carriages on published studio reformers run about 56–67 cm wide and 89–93 cm long, and the mats sold for them range from 54.5 × 94 cm to 100 × 61 cm. Measure the carriage width, carriage length and shoulder-rest area, then order a mat cut to that template. SANFAN makes reformer mats to your machine template from 100 pcs.

Why one mat does not fit every reformer

A reformer mat sits on the carriage as a non-slip, wipe-clean layer between the client and the upholstery. Carriages differ by brand and model, so a mat cut for one machine can be loose, short, or blocked by the shoulder rests on another. Grip Mat Studio, a reformer-mat seller, states that its mats are “not a universal one-size-fits-all product” and that even reformers from the same maker can differ by model and year.

How to measure your carriage: 3 steps

  • 1. Carriage width. Measure the usable width of the carriage between the side rails and compare it with the mat width.
  • 2. Carriage length. Measure the carriage length and decide how much coverage you want, including the headrest end.
  • 3. Shoulder-rest and headrest area. Note the spacing between the shoulder rests and the headrest section, because a mat that ignores them will not lie flat.

Then send us the brand and model, the three measurements and a photo of the carriage. For an unusual shape, a paper or card template is the most reliable input.

FAQ

What size is a Pilates reformer mat?

There is no single size. Reformer mats on the market range from about 54.5 x 94 cm (a contoured mat with a headrest section) to 100 x 61 cm (a 5 mm mat made for one specific reformer). The right size is the one cut to your carriage.

How do I measure my reformer carriage for a mat?

Measure three things: the carriage width, the carriage length, and the shoulder-rest and headrest area. Compare them with the mat's overall width and length, and send us a photo and a template if the machine has an unusual shape.

Do reformer mats fit every reformer brand?

No. Carriage sizes differ by brand, by model and sometimes by production year. For example, Align-Pilates lists carriages from 56 x 89 cm (R8-Pro) to 67 x 91 cm (A8-Pro), so a mat made for one will be loose or short on another.

How thick should a reformer mat be?
