TPE vs PU Rubber Yoga Mats: Which to Choose in 2026

Choosing the right material is the single biggest decision for a private-label yoga mat. Here is how TPE and PU/rubber stack up this year — from a factory that makes both.

What is TPE?

TPE (thermoplastic elastomer) is a lightweight, recyclable, odorless material. Most private-label TPE mats use a double-layer construction — a dense base for support and a textured top for grip — in thicknesses of 4–8 mm. It contains no PVC and no harmful plasticizers.

What is PU / rubber?

A PU mat pairs a polyurethane top layer with a natural-rubber base. The result is exceptional grip, even when wet, which is why it dominates hot-yoga and premium studio lines. The trade-off is weight and a higher unit cost.

Cost and MOQ

TPE is more cost-efficient and friendlier to low MOQs (we start TPE at 100 pcs per design/color). PU/rubber carries a premium price and usually a higher minimum, so it suits established or premium lines.

Grip and use case

TPE gives reliable dry grip for everyday home and gym practice. PU/rubber is the choice for hot yoga, sweaty sessions and studios where slip is not an option.

If you are launching a new brand, TPE is the safer first product. Add a PU/rubber premium line once demand is proven.

Eco profile

TPE is recyclable and PVC-free. Natural rubber is renewable but not recyclable; PU is synthetic. For sustainability messaging, we can advise on recyclable TPE formulations.

Which should your brand choose?

  • New brand / mass market: start with TPE — lower risk, lower cost.
  • Premium studio line: PU/rubber for grip and positioning.
  • Most growing brands: run both, let the market decide.

TPE 与 PU 橡胶瑜伽垫：2026 年怎么选

对自有品牌瑜伽垫来说，选对材质是最关键的一步。今年 TPE 与 PU 橡胶孰优孰劣？这是一家两种都生产的工厂给出的实情。

什么是 TPE？

TPE（热塑性弹性体）是一种轻质、可回收、无气味的材料。多数自有品牌 TPE 垫采用双层结构——高密度底层提供支撑，纹理面层提供抓地，厚度通常为 4–8 毫米。不含 PVC，也不含害增塑剂。

什么是 PU / 橡胶？

PU 垫将聚氨酯面层与天然橡胶底层结合，带来极佳的抓地力，即便出汗也不滑，因此主导高温瑜伽与高端工作室产品线。代价是更重、单件成本更高。

成本与起订量

TPE 更具成本优势，也更友好于低起订（我们 TPE 按 100 片/款/色起订）。PU/橡胶价格更高，通常最低起订量也更高，更适合成熟或高端产品线。

抓地力与适用场景

TPE 在日常居家与健身房干爽练习中抓地可靠。PU/橡胶是高温瑜伽、大汗练习与工作室的首选，绝不打滑。

如果正在推新品牌，TPE 是更稳妥的首款产品。需求验证后，再上 PU/橡胶高端线。

环保属性

TPE 可回收、无 PVC。天然橡胶可再生但不可回收；PU 为合成材料。若主打可持续，我们可推荐可回收 TPE 配方。

你的品牌该选哪个？

  • 新品牌 / 大众市场：从 TPE 起步——风险与成本更低。
  • 高端工作室线：选 PU/橡胶，抓地力与定位兼具。
  • 多数成长型品牌：两条线并行，让市场来选。
