REACH, CA Prop 65 & 6P: Yoga Mat Rules for US/EU

Selling into the US or EU means meeting chemical-safety rules. Here is the short list buyers ask about — and the proof a compliant factory should provide.

United States: CA Prop 65 + 6P/7P

California's Prop 65 limits certain chemicals, and US buyers watch phthalate limits (the "6P/7P" list: DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP, DNOP and often DnHP). Mats must be free of these above threshold levels.

European Union: REACH

REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals) restricts Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC). Compliant materials are selected and tested so mats are safe for the EU market.

FAQ

Is REACH the same as California Proposition 65?

No. REACH is EU legislation governing chemical substances in products placed on the European market. California Proposition 65 is a state law requiring warnings for listed chemicals. They have different substance lists and different compliance mechanics, so a mat can satisfy one and still fail the other.

Do I need separate testing for each colour?

In most cases yes — pigments are a common source of restricted substances, and a report covering a natural or black mat does not automatically cover a brightly coloured one. Budget for testing per colour family, and ask your supplier which colours share a formulation.

How often should test reports be renewed?

Most buyers and marketplaces treat a report as current for 12 months, but any change in material, supplier or pigment invalidates it. Ask for a fresh report whenever the specification changes, not just when the calendar rolls over.

What is the difference between 6P and 7P?

Both refer to phthalate restriction lists. 6P covers six common phthalates; 7P adds a seventh. Buyers selling into the EU typically ask for 6P or 7P free test reports as evidence that plasticisers are not present above permitted limits.

REACH、CA Prop 65 与 6P：出口欧美的瑜伽垫合规

销往美国或欧盟，就必须满足化学品安全规则。这是采购商常问的清单——以及合规工厂应提供的证据。

美国：CA Prop 65 与 6P/7P

加州 Prop 65 限制特定化学品，美国买家关注邻苯二甲酸盐限值（"6P/7P"清单：DEHP、DBP、BBP、DINP、DIDP、DNOP，常含 DnHP）。瑜伽垫中这些物质须低于阈值。

欧盟：REACH

REACH（化学品注册、评估、授权与限制法规）限制高关注物质（SVHC）。合规材料经筛选与检测，确保瑜伽垫可安全进入欧盟市场。

FAQ

REACH 和加州 65 号提案是一回事吗？

不是。REACH 是欧盟法规，管控投放欧洲市场产品中的化学物质；加州 65 号提案是州法，要求对所列化学物质做警示。两者管控清单与合规机制不同，一张垫子可能满足其中之一却仍不符合另一个。

每个颜色都要单独检测吗？

多数情况下需要——色粉是受限物质的常见来源，覆盖本色或黑色垫的报告不会自动覆盖鲜艳色。请按色系预算检测费用，并询问供应商哪些颜色共用同一配方。

检测报告多久要更新？

多数买家与平台认可报告 12 个月内有效，但材料、供应商或色粉一旦变更，报告即失效。规格变更时就应索要新报告，而不是等日历翻页。

6P 和 7P 有什么区别？

两者都是邻苯二甲酸酯的受限清单。6P 管控六种常见邻苯，7P 多一项。销往欧盟的买家通常要求 6P 或 7P 检测报告，以证明增塑剂未超出允许限值。
