REACH, CA Prop 65 & 6P: Yoga Mat Rules for US/EU

Selling into the US or EU means meeting chemical-safety rules that most buyers only discover when a retailer or customs officer asks for paperwork. This guide covers the tests that actually get requested, what a valid report looks like, and how to keep compliance holding batch after batch.

Two frameworks dominate yoga mat compliance. For the United States, California Proposition 65 requires warnings for listed chemicals, and US buyers screen mats against the 6P/7P phthalate lists (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP, DNOP and often DnHP) — plasticizers that matter mainly for PVC, which is one reason most US brands have left PVC behind. For the European Union, REACH restricts Substances of Very High Concern (SVHC), and the candidate list is updated regularly, so a declaration from two years ago is not a declaration for this year. Proof works the same on both sides of the Atlantic: a current third-party test report (SGS, TÜV, Intertek) that names your material, your colour and a recent date, backed by batch traceability that links every production lot to its formulation. Claims are cheap; documents with dates and lab names are not.

United States: CA Prop 65 and the 6P/7P phthalate list

California Proposition 65 is a state law requiring businesses to warn consumers about significant exposures to chemicals the state has listed as causing cancer, birth defects or other reproductive harm. For yoga mats the practical exposure question is plasticizers. The "6P" list covers six phthalates — DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP and DNOP — and "7P" adds DnHP. PVC foam needs plasticizers to stay flexible, which is why the phthalate conversation and the PVC material conversation are the same conversation. TPE and rubber constructions sidestep most of it, and our test reports cover both routes.

Compliance mechanics differ from the EU, too: Prop 65 is a warning-labelling regime, not a ban. Some brands reformulate to avoid the warning label entirely; others label. That is a commercial decision, but it needs to be made before the packaging prints, not after.

European Union: REACH and SVHC

REACH (Registration, Evaluation, Authorisation and Restriction of Chemicals) governs chemical substances in products placed on the EU market. The part that touches yoga mats is the SVHC candidate list — Substances of Very High Concern — which is updated roughly twice a year. A material that cleared the list in 2024 can appear on it in 2026. This is why we keep formulations locked and watch the list on a schedule: when an update touches a compound we use, the affected batch gets flagged before it ships, not after it is held at customs. Buyers selling children-adjacent ranges should also ask about EN 71.

How to read a test report in 60 seconds

A report is only useful if three things check out. First, the product description matches your SKU — a report for "TPE mat, purple, 6 mm" does not cover your black 8 mm (5/16") mat. Second, the tested sample matches your specification in material and colour, because pigments are a common source of restricted substances. Third, the date is current; most buyers and marketplaces treat reports as valid for 12 months, and any formulation change invalidates one immediately. A generic "passed" stamp without those details is decoration. We send the full document, not a summary line, so your team can verify every field before the goods move.

Staying compliant batch to batch

Compliance breaks when formulations drift, and drift happens when a factory quietly substitutes a cheaper compound. We keep material recipes locked and batch-traceable, so the mat shipped in month six matches the certified sample from month one. When a regulation tightens, as the REACH SVHC list does on a schedule, we flag affected formulations before they reach your order. Ask any supplier how they handle a mid-year SVHC update; the answer tells you how the next two years of your product line will go.

FAQ

Is REACH the same as California Proposition 65?

No. REACH is EU legislation governing chemical substances in products placed on the European market. California Proposition 65 is a state law requiring warnings for listed chemicals. They have different substance lists and different compliance mechanics, so a mat can satisfy one and still fail the other.

Do I need separate testing for each colour?

In most cases yes — pigments are a common source of restricted substances, and a report covering a natural or black mat does not automatically cover a brightly coloured one. Budget for testing per colour family, and ask your supplier which colours share a formulation.

How often should test reports be renewed?

Most buyers and marketplaces treat a report as current for 12 months, but any change in material, supplier or pigment invalidates it. Ask for a fresh report whenever the specification changes, not just when the calendar rolls over.

What is the difference between 6P and 7P?

Both refer to phthalate restriction lists. 6P covers six common phthalates (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DINP, DIDP, DNOP); 7P adds DnHP. Buyers selling into the EU and US typically ask for 6P or 7P free test reports as evidence that plasticisers are not present above permitted limits.

REACH、加州 65 号提案与 6P：欧美瑜伽垫合规规则

进美国或欧盟市场，就要过化学品安全这一关——而多数采购是在零售商或海关要文件时才第一次面对它。这篇讲清真正会被要求检测什么、有效报告长什么样、合规怎么一批一批守得住。

瑜伽垫合规主要由两套框架主导。美国方面，加州 65 号提案要求对列入清单的化学品做警示，美国采购方还会按 6P／7P 邻苯二甲酸酯清单筛查（DEHP、DBP、BBP、DINP、DIDP、DNOP，往往再加上 DnHP）——这类增塑剂主要与 PVC 相关，也是多数美国品牌放弃 PVC 的原因之一。欧盟方面，REACH 限制高关注物质（SVHC），而候选清单定期更新，所以两年前的声明不等于今年的声明。两岸的证明方式是一样的：一份现行的第三方检测报告（SGS、TÜV、Intertek），写清你的材料、你的颜色和近期日期，再加能把每个生产批号对应到配方的批次追溯。宣称很便宜，带日期和实验室名字的文件不便宜。

美国：加州 65 号提案与 6P／7P 邻苯二甲酸酯

加州 65 号提案是一部州法，要求企业对消费者可能接触到的、被州政府列为致癌、致畸或其他生殖毒性的化学品做出警示。落到瑜伽垫上，实际要谈的是增塑剂。"6P"覆盖六种邻苯二甲酸酯——DEHP、DBP、BBP、DINP、DIDP、DNOP，"7P"再加上 DnHP。PVC 发泡要靠增塑剂保持柔软，所以"邻苯二甲酸酯"这个话题和"PVC 材质"这个话题，其实是同一个话题。TPE 和橡胶结构基本绕开了它，两条路线的检测报告我们都能出。

执行机制也和欧盟不同：65 号提案是警示标签制度，不是禁令。有的品牌改配方，为的是完全不用贴警示；有的品牌选择贴。这是商业决策，但必须在包装开印之前定下来，不是之后。

欧盟：REACH 与 SVHC

REACH（化学品注册、评估、授权和限制）管的是投放欧盟市场产品中的化学物质。落到瑜伽垫上的是 SVHC 候选清单——高关注物质——大约每半年更新一次。2024 年能过清单的材料，2026 年可能被列进去。所以我们把配方锁死并定期盯清单：一旦更新碰到我们在用的化合物，受影响的批次会在发货前就被标记出来，而不是等卡在海关之后。做儿童相关品类的采购，还应多问一句 EN 71。

60 秒看懂一份检测报告

一份报告，只有三处对得上才有用。第一，产品描述要对得上你的 SKU——"TPE 垫、紫色、6 mm"的报告不覆盖你的黑色 8 mm（5/16"）垫。第二，送检样品要在材料和颜色上对得上你的规格，因为颜料是受限物质的常见来源。第三，日期要现行；多数采购方和电商平台把报告有效期按 12 个月算，而任何配方变更都会立刻让它失效。没有这些细节、只有一个"通过"章的，是装饰品。我们发完整文件，不是一行摘要，让你的人在货动之前逐项核对。

合规崩，往往崩在配方漂移上，而配方漂移通常是工厂悄悄换成了更便宜的原料。我们把材料配方锁死并做批次追溯，所以第 6 个月出的垫子和第 1 个月认证过的样品是同一个东西。当法规收紧——REACH 的 SVHC 清单就是按日程收紧的——我们会在受影响的配方进到你的订单之前就标出来。你可以问任何供应商一句：年中来了 SVHC 更新，你们怎么处理？这个答案基本能告诉你，你这条产品线接下来两年会怎么走。

常见问题

REACH 和加州 65 号提案是一回事吗？

不是。REACH 是管欧盟市场产品中化学物质的欧盟法规；加州 65 号提案是要求对清单化学品做警示的州法。两者的物质清单和执行机制都不同，所以一张垫子可能过了一个、却过不了另一个。

每个颜色都要单独做检测吗？

多数情况要。颜料是受限物质的常见来源，一份覆盖本白或黑色垫子的报告，不会自动覆盖高饱和彩色的那一张。按色系预留检测预算，并问清供应商哪些颜色共用同一配方。

检测报告多久更新一次？

多数采购方和电商平台把 12 个月内的报告视为现行，但材料、供应商或颜料的任何变更都会立刻让它失效。规格一变就要新报告，而不是等日历翻年。

6P 和 7P 有什么区别？

两者都是邻苯二甲酸酯的限制清单。6P 覆盖六种常见邻苯二甲酸酯（DEHP、DBP、BBP、DINP、DIDP、DNOP），7P 再加上 DnHP。销往欧美市场的采购通常要求 6P 或 7P 合格的检测报告，作为增塑剂未超限值的证据。
