Jump Rope Guide跳绳选型

Building a jump rope range that sells怎么配一条好卖的跳绳产品线

There are three rope constructions and they are not interchangeable. PVC-coated cord is the volume seller — comfortable if it clips your shins, and fine for schools and general retail. Steel-wire rope is faster and takes far more wear, which is what competitive and CrossFit customers pay for. Beaded rope holds a visible arc in the air, so a beginner can see the rhythm instead of guessing at it. Name the customer and we will put the right core in front of you first.跳绳有三种绳芯结构，互不替代。PVC 包覆绳是走量款 —— 打到小腿也不疼，学校与大众零售都合适；钢丝绳更快、耐用得多，竞技与 CrossFit 客户就买这个；珠节绳在空中能拉出可见的弧线，新手看得见节奏，不用靠猜。先说清楚卖给谁，我们就把对的那一种先推给你。

Counting splits the range just as sharply. Mechanical counters survive drops and need no battery; LCD counters give a clean readout and are the default for retail gift boxes; cordless builds have no rope at all, so someone can train in a flat without hitting the ceiling or the furniture. Handles run from a 12 cm kids' grip to a 19.3 cm adult format, so the same rope can be built for a child or for a competition.计数方式同样把产品线分得很开。机械计数器摔不坏、不用电池；LCD 计数读数清楚，是零售礼盒的默认选择；无绳款干脆没有绳子，在公寓里练也不会打到天花板或家具。握柄从 12 cm 的儿童款到 19.3 cm 的成人款，同一根绳可以做成给小孩的，也可以做成给比赛的。

Rope length, grip colour, print and packaging are all set per order, and the counter housing can carry your logo. Kids' and adult formats come off the same line, so a school or a gym chain can order a mixed box as long as each variant meets the minimum. MOQ is 100 pcs, with sampling in about 7 days.绳长、握把颜色、印刷与包装全部按订单定，计数器的外壳上可以放你的 Logo。儿童款与成人款同一条线生产，所以学校或连锁健身房可以订混装箱，只要每个款式各满起订量。100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Jump rope questions buyers ask买家会问的跳绳问题

Which rope core should we choose?绳芯该怎么选？
PVC-coated cord for general retail and schools, steel wire for speed and competition, and beaded cord for beginners who need to see the arc. If you sell to more than one of those groups, order a sample of each and let the sell-through decide which one deserves the shelf space.PVC 包覆绳面向大众零售与学校，钢丝绳面向速度与竞技，珠节绳给需要看见弧线的新手。如果这几类客户你都在做，就各打一份样，让实际动销决定哪一个值得占货架。
How many counting options are there?有几种计数方式？
Three: mechanical, LCD and smart. Mechanical counters take the knocks and never need charging. LCD models are the retail default because the readout sells itself on a shelf. Cordless models have no rope at all, so they work in a flat where a full-length rope would hit the ceiling.三种：机械、LCD 与智能。机械计数器经摔，永远不用充电；LCD 款是零售默认选择，因为读数在货架上自己会说话；无绳款完全没有绳子，所以在一根整绳会打到天花板的公寓里照样练。
Who buys the cordless version?无绳款卖给谁？
Apartment and hotel customers, and anyone rehabilitating a joint who has been told to avoid impact. Because there is no rope, length stops being a constraint. The handle we build for this format is 19.3 cm long and 3.1 cm across.公寓住户、酒店客户，以及被叮嘱避免冲击、正在做关节康复的人。因为没有绳子，绳长就不再是限制。这一款我们做的握柄长 19.3 cm、直径 3.1 cm。
Can the rope length and the grips be customised?绳长和握把能定制吗？
Yes. Rope length is cut per order, grips run from a 12 cm kids' handle to a 14.8 cm or 19.3 cm adult format, and the grip colour plus printed logo are set with the rest of the order. The counter housing can carry a logo as well.可以。绳长按订单裁，握柄从 12 cm 儿童款到 14.8 cm、19.3 cm 成人款都有，握把颜色与印刷 Logo 随整单一起定。计数器的外壳上也能放 Logo。
What is the MOQ and what packing do you offer?起订量和包装是什么情况？
MOQ is 100 pcs per variant, sampling in about 7 days. Rope, handles and packaging are produced in one run, and retail boxes, pouches and colour-matched sets are all available.每个款式 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。绳子、握柄与包装一次生产完成，零售盒、收纳袋与配色套装都能做。
