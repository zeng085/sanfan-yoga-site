Jump Rope Guide

Building a jump rope range that sells

There are three rope constructions and they are not interchangeable. PVC-coated cord is the volume seller — comfortable if it clips your shins, and fine for schools and general retail. Steel-wire rope is faster and takes far more wear, which is what competitive and CrossFit customers pay for. Beaded rope holds a visible arc in the air, so a beginner can see the rhythm instead of guessing at it. Name the customer and we will put the right core in front of you first.

Counting splits the range just as sharply. Mechanical counters survive drops and need no battery; LCD counters give a clean readout and are the default for retail gift boxes; cordless builds have no rope at all, so someone can train in a flat without hitting the ceiling or the furniture. Handles run from a 12 cm kids' grip to a 19.3 cm adult format, so the same rope can be built for a child or for a competition.

Rope length, grip colour, print and packaging are all set per order, and the counter housing can carry your logo. Kids' and adult formats come off the same line, so a school or a gym chain can order a mixed box as long as each variant meets the minimum. MOQ is 500 pcs, with sampling in about 7 days.

FAQ

Jump rope questions buyers ask

Which rope core should we choose?
PVC-coated cord for general retail and schools, steel wire for speed and competition, and beaded cord for beginners who need to see the arc. If you sell to more than one of those groups, order a sample of each and let the sell-through decide which one deserves the shelf space.
How many counting options are there?
Three: mechanical, LCD and smart. Mechanical counters take the knocks and never need charging. LCD models are the retail default because the readout sells itself on a shelf. Cordless models have no rope at all, so they work in a flat where a full-length rope would hit the ceiling.
Who buys the cordless version?
Apartment and hotel customers, and anyone rehabilitating a joint who has been told to avoid impact. Because there is no rope, length stops being a constraint. The handle we build for this format is 19.3 cm long and 3.1 cm across.
Can the rope length and the grips be customised?
Yes. Rope length is cut per order, grips run from a 12 cm kids' handle to a 14.8 cm or 19.3 cm adult format, and the grip colour plus printed logo are set with the rest of the order. The counter housing can carry a logo as well.
What is the MOQ and what packing do you offer?
MOQ is 500 pcs per variant, sampling in about 7 days. Rope, handles and packaging are produced in one run, and retail boxes, pouches and colour-matched sets are all available.
