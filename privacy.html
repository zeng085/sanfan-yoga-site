Privacy Policy

In plain language: what we collect, who receives it, and what you can demand.

1. Who is responsible

FUJIAN SANFAN Sports Products Co., Ltd., No. 8 Haiguang Road, Yuejin Industrial Zone, Xibin Town, Jinjiang, Quanzhou, Fujian, China. Contact: zenglinggun@gmail.com

2. What we collect when you send an inquiry

If you use the inquiry form we receive the data you enter: name, company, email address, product of interest, estimated quantity and message. We use this data solely to answer your inquiry and to prepare a quotation.

The form is delivered through Formspree (formspree.io, United States), which acts as our technical processor. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

3. Website usage data (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are used and how the site performs. It is loaded only after you consent in the consent banner at the bottom of the screen. If you decline, no analytics data is collected at all and the site works exactly the same. IP addresses are anonymised by Google.

4. Cookies and local storage

We set no advertising cookies. The only value stored in your browser is your consent decision, in localStorage under the key sanfan_consent. You can remove it at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

5. Hosting

This site is served as a static site from Vercel (vercel.com, United States). The hosting provider processes request data (time, requested file, IP address) for delivery, security and error analysis.

6. Your rights under the GDPR

You may request access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, objection to processing, and portability. Send any request to zenglinggun@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (in Germany: the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Freedom of Information of the state where the company is based).

7. No data selling

We do not sell, rent out or trade personal data, and we share nothing with advertising networks.
