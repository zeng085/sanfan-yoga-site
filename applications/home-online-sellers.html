How to buy

How experienced buyers order this range

For marketplace sellers the winning order is small and often: 100–300 pcs per SKU, three or four SKUs per shipment, replenished as listings prove out. Unit weight is the number to optimise — a jump rope or a band set ships at under 300 g, which is where the marketplace fee maths works. Order the compliance pack with the first shipment: polybag warning text, FNSKU labels and the available test reports (RoHS 2.0 for our TPE, PU rubber and suede rubber mats), so nothing has to be re-labelled in a three-week warehouse window.

FAQ

Home & Online Sellers — buyer questions

What is the real MOQ if we want to test several colours?
100 pcs per SKU per colour. You can split one order across several colours and several SKUs as long as you reach a shipable weight — most sellers start with 3–5 SKUs at 100–200 pcs each.
Do you supply FBA-ready packaging and labels?
Yes. We produce polybags with the required warning text, apply barcodes and FNSKU labels, and pack to Amazon carton specs. Tell us the marketplace and we will match the current requirement rather than a two-year-old spec sheet.
How fast can you ship a first small order?
Sampling 7 days; a 100–300 pcs per SKU order is typically produced in 10–15 days and we ship by air or express if you need to catch a launch. Export documents and courier booking are handled on our side.
