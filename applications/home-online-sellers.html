How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

For marketplace sellers the winning order is small and often: 100–300 pcs per SKU, three or four SKUs per shipment, replenished as listings prove out. Unit weight is the number to optimise — a jump rope or a band set ships at under 300 g, which is where the marketplace fee maths works. Order the compliance pack with the first shipment: polybag warning text, FNSKU labels and the ROHS / REACH report, so nothing has to be re-labelled in a three-week warehouse window.对平台卖家来说，最优的订单是「小批量、高频率」：单款 100–300 片，每批三四个 SKU，哪个 listing 跑出来就补哪个。要优化的核心数字是单件重量 —— 跳绳或弹力带套装单件不到 300 g，平台费用的账才算得过来。第一批发货就要一起订合规包：胶袋警示文字、FNSKU 标签与 ROHS / REACH 报告，免得在只有三周的入仓窗口里还要重新贴标。

FAQ常见问题

Home & Online Sellers — buyer questions家庭用户与电商卖家 —— 买家常问

What is the real MOQ if we want to test several colours?想试几个颜色的话，真实起订量是多少？
100 pcs per SKU per colour. You can split one order across several colours and several SKUs as long as you reach a shipable weight — most sellers start with 3–5 SKUs at 100–200 pcs each.单款单色 100 片起订。一个订单可以在多个颜色与多个 SKU 之间拆分，只要凑够可发运重量 —— 多数卖家首批做 3–5 款、每款 100–200 片。
Do you supply FBA-ready packaging and labels?你们提供可直接入 FBA 的包装与标签吗？
Yes. We produce polybags with the required warning text, apply barcodes and FNSKU labels, and pack to Amazon carton specs. Tell us the marketplace and we will match the current requirement rather than a two-year-old spec sheet.提供。我们做带规定警示文字的胶袋、贴条码与 FNSKU 标签，并按亚马逊外箱规格装箱。告诉我们具体站点，我们按当前要求执行，而不是照两年前的旧规格。
How fast can you ship a first small order?首批小单多久能发货？
Sampling 7 days; a 100–300 pcs per SKU order is typically produced in 10–15 days and we ship by air or express if you need to catch a launch. Export documents and courier booking are handled on our side.打样 7 天；单款 100–300 片的订单通常 10–15 天完成生产，赶上线可以走空运或快递。出口单证与快递订舱由我们这边处理。
