How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

Studios usually order in two waves: a fit-out wave (mats for the room, props per class, hammocks or reformers) and a reorder wave (replacing worn member mats once a year). Order both in the same colour system the first time — reorders match exactly because we keep your colour and logo files on record. Tell us your class count and room size and we will come back with a BOM (bill of materials) and a suggested split between teacher-grade and member-grade mats.瑜伽馆通常分两批下单：开业配置批（教室垫、按班配辅具、吊床或器械）与补货批（会员垫每年替换一次）。第一次就把两批放进同一套配色体系 —— 我们会存档你的颜色与 Logo 文件，之后的补货能做到完全一致。告诉我们教室数量与面积，我们会回一份配置清单（BOM），并建议教练级与会员级垫子的配比。

FAQ常见问题

Yoga & Pilates Studios — buyer questions瑜伽馆与普拉提工作室 —— 买家常问

Can you supply a complete studio fit-out, not just mats?你们能配齐整个馆，而不只是垫子吗？
Yes. Mats, blocks, straps, bolsters, Pilates rings, mini balls, reformer mats, aerial hammocks and bags all come from our own plants, so one PO covers the room. We ship them together in one container, which is usually 15–25% cheaper than sourcing each item separately.可以。垫类、瑜伽砖、伸展带、抱枕、普拉提圈、小瑜伽球、器械垫、空中吊床与包袋都出自我们自有工厂，一张订单就能覆盖整间教室，同柜出货通常比分开采购省 15–25%。
What is the MOQ for a studio order?瑜伽馆订单的起订量是多少？
100 pcs per SKU, and you can mix SKUs to reach a container. A typical boutique studio order is 40–60 mats plus 20 prop sets; a chain order is 300–800 mats. Sampling takes 7 days with your logo and colour.单款 100 片起订，可多款拼柜。精品馆通常订 40–60 张垫 + 20 套辅具；连锁馆在 300–800 张。打样 7 天，可带你的 Logo 与颜色。
Can you match our existing studio colours?能按我们现有的会馆配色做吗？
Send a Pantone code, a fabric swatch or even a photo — we match the mat surface, the block and the bag to it, and keep the recipe on file so every reorder is identical. Double-colour mats and custom block colours are standard, not special.发 Pantone 号、色卡甚至照片都行 —— 我们把垫面、瑜伽砖与包袋统一到同一个色，并归档配方，保证每次补货完全一致。双色垫与定制砖色都是常规能力，不是特例。
