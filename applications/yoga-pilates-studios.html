How to buy

How experienced buyers order this range

Studios usually order in two waves: a fit-out wave (mats for the room, props per class, hammocks or reformers) and a reorder wave (replacing worn member mats once a year). Order both in the same colour system the first time — reorders match exactly because we keep your colour and logo files on record. Tell us your class count and room size and we will come back with a BOM (bill of materials) and a suggested split between teacher-grade and member-grade mats.

FAQ

Yoga & Pilates Studios — buyer questions

Can you supply a complete studio fit-out, not just mats?
Yes. Mats, blocks, straps, bolsters, Pilates rings, mini balls, reformer mats, aerial hammocks and bags all come from our own plants, so one PO covers the room. We ship them together in one container, which is usually 15–25% cheaper than sourcing each item separately.
What is the MOQ for a studio order?
100 pcs per SKU, and you can mix SKUs to reach a container. A typical boutique studio order is 40–60 mats plus 20 prop sets; a chain order is 300–800 mats. Sampling takes 7 days with your logo and colour.
Can you match our existing studio colours?
Send a Pantone code, a fabric swatch or even a photo — we match the mat surface, the block and the bag to it, and keep the recipe on file so every reorder is identical. Double-colour mats and custom block colours are standard, not special.
