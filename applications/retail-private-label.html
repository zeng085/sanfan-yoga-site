How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

Private label is mostly a sequencing problem. Start with two hero SKUs that prove the colour and the logo technique, add fillers in the second order, then consolidate packaging so one carton size serves several SKUs — that alone typically cuts freight 8–12%. We keep your colour recipe, logo files and packaging artwork on record, so every season's order is a repeat, not a re-setup. Ask for the compliance pack on the first order; importers always want it eventually.贴牌本质上是排序问题。先用 2 个主推款验证颜色与 Logo 工艺，第二单再补品类款，然后合并包装规格让一个箱型覆盖多个 SKU —— 仅这一项通常就能降 8–12% 运费。我们会归档你的色号配方、Logo 文件与包装稿件，所以每一季都是「复单」而不是「重新开一次」。第一单就要合规资料包，进口商迟早会要。

FAQ常见问题

Retail & Private Label — buyer questions零售与贴牌 —— 买家常问

What is the minimum order for a private-label run?贴牌起订量是多少？
100 pcs per SKU, mixable across the range so you can test a whole shelf cheaply. Sampling is 7 days with your logo and colour. There is no separate artwork or tooling fee on standard logo techniques.单款 100 片起订，可跨品类混订，方便低成本试整层货架。打样 7 天，可带你的 Logo 与颜色。常规 Logo 工艺不收单独的稿件费或开模费。
Can you develop an entirely new product from our idea?能按我们的想法开发全新产品吗？
Yes — that is the ODM half. Send a sketch, a competitor sample or just a target price and use case; our R&D team comes back with materials, tooling cost and a physical sample, usually within 7 days.可以，这正是 ODM 的部分。发草图、竞品样品，或只给目标价与使用场景都行；研发团队会给出材质方案、开模成本与实物样品，通常 7 天内。
Will you send the test reports our marketplace requires?能提供平台要求的检测报告吗？
Yes. We hold ISO 9001 and BSCI audits plus SGS testing, and can arrange ROHS / CE / REACH reports for your specific materials and colours — including whatever additional testing Amazon or your importer asks for.可以。我们有 ISO 9001 与 BSCI 审核，以及 SGS 检测，并能针对你的具体材质与颜色出 ROHS / CE / REACH 报告 —— 包括亚马逊或进口商额外要求的项目。
