How to buy

How experienced buyers order this range

Private label is mostly a sequencing problem. Start with two hero SKUs that prove the colour and the logo technique, add fillers in the second order, then consolidate packaging so one carton size serves several SKUs — that alone typically cuts freight 8–12%. We keep your colour recipe, logo files and packaging artwork on record, so every season's order is a repeat, not a re-setup. Ask for the compliance pack on the first order; importers always want it eventually.

FAQ

Retail & Private Label — buyer questions

What is the minimum order for a private-label run?
100 pcs per SKU, mixable across the range so you can test a whole shelf cheaply. Sampling is 7 days with your logo and colour. There is no separate artwork or tooling fee on standard logo techniques.
Can you develop an entirely new product from our idea?
Yes — that is the ODM half. Send a sketch, a competitor sample or just a target price and use case; our R&D team comes back with materials, tooling cost and a physical sample, usually within 7 days.
Will you send the test reports our marketplace requires?
Yes. We hold ISO 9001 and BSCI audits plus SGS testing, and can arrange ROHS / CE / REACH reports for your specific materials and colours — including whatever additional testing Amazon or your importer asks for.
