How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

Hospitality buyers care about three things our specs answer directly: material odour (TPE and natural rubber are closed-cell and low-VOC, so a mat does not scent a wardrobe), cleaning (closed-cell surfaces can be wiped with a standard hotel sanitiser) and rolling diameter (a 6 mm mat rolls to roughly 16 cm, which fits standard wardrobe shelving). Tell us your room count and whether the kit is per-room or per wellness floor, and we will quote both a full-house rollout and a 10% housekeeping float.酒店采购最在意的三点，我们的规格都能直接回答：材质气味（TPE 与天然橡胶是闭孔低挥发材质，垫子不会给衣柜留味）、清洁（闭孔表面可用酒店常规消毒剂擦拭）、卷径（6 mm 垫卷起约 16 cm，能放进标准衣柜层板）。告诉我们房间数、以及套装是按房配还是按康体楼层配，我们会同时报「全店铺开」与「10% 客房部周转备品」两套价格。

FAQ常见问题

Hotels, Resorts & Spas — buyer questions酒店、度假村与 SPA —— 买家常问

Will the mats smell in a closed wardrobe?垫子放在密闭衣柜里会有味道吗？
Our TPE and natural-rubber mats are closed-cell and low-VOC — no plasticiser smell, which is why hospitality buyers specify them over PVC. PVC mats are available for budget projects but we will tell you when they are the wrong choice.我们的 TPE 与天然橡胶垫为闭孔低挥发材质，没有增塑剂气味 —— 这正是酒店客户指定它们而不是 PVC 的原因。预算型项目也可以做 PVC，但如果是错的选择我们会直接说明。
Can you emboss our hotel crest on the mat?能在垫子上压印酒店店徽吗？
Yes — embossing, laser engraving or full-colour printing. Logo files are kept on record, so a reorder in two years produces an identical mat. Tooling for embossing is usually free above 300 pcs.可以 —— 压印、激光雕刻或全彩印花都行。Logo 文件我们会归档，两年后补货做出来的垫子完全一致。压印模具在 300 片以上通常免费。
Do you ship to hotel projects on a staged schedule?酒店项目能分批交货吗？
Yes. Many projects ship in two or three stages to match the opening schedule — we hold produced stock and release it against your dates, and we handle export documents for FOB, CIF or DDP terms.可以。很多项目按开业节点分两到三批交货 —— 我们备好库存，按你的日期放货，并按 FOB、CIF 或 DDP 条款办理出口单证。
