How to buy

How experienced buyers order this range

Hospitality buyers care about three things our specs answer directly: material odour (TPE and natural rubber are closed-cell and low-VOC, so a mat does not scent a wardrobe), cleaning (closed-cell surfaces can be wiped with a standard hotel sanitiser) and rolling diameter (a 6 mm mat rolls to roughly 16 cm, which fits standard wardrobe shelving). Tell us your room count and whether the kit is per-room or per wellness floor, and we will quote both a full-house rollout and a 10% housekeeping float.

FAQ

Hotels, Resorts & Spas — buyer questions

Will the mats smell in a closed wardrobe?
Our TPE and natural-rubber mats are closed-cell and low-VOC — no plasticiser smell, which is why hospitality buyers specify them over PVC. PVC mats are available for budget projects but we will tell you when they are the wrong choice.
Can you emboss our hotel crest on the mat?
Yes — embossing, laser engraving or full-colour printing. Logo files are kept on record, so a reorder in two years produces an identical mat. Tooling for embossing is usually free above 300 pcs.
Do you ship to hotel projects on a staged schedule?
Yes. Many projects ship in two or three stages to match the opening schedule — we hold produced stock and release it against your dates, and we handle export documents for FOB, CIF or DDP terms.
