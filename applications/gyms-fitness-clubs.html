How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

Gyms buy in three buckets: capital equipment at fit-out, consumables on an annual cycle (ropes, bands, mats) and retail stock at the front desk. The single biggest saving is ordering all three from one factory on one colour reference — no mismatch between the dumbbell rack and the mats, no three different lead times. We can also laser-engrave your club logo into the silicone weights and mats at no tooling cost.健身房采购通常分三块：开业时的固定资产、年度循环的耗材（跳绳、弹力带、垫子）、以及前台的零售库存。最大的省钱点是把三块放进同一家工厂、同一个色号 —— 哑铃架与地垫不会色差，也不用等三个交期。硅胶配重与地垫上的会所 Logo 可激光雕刻，不收开模费。

FAQ常见问题

Gyms & Fitness Clubs — buyer questions健身房与团课俱乐部 —— 买家常问

Do the silicone weights chip or peel with daily gym use?硅胶配重日常高频使用会掉漆、脱皮吗？
No. The skin is a single moulded silicone shell over a cast-iron core — there is no paint layer to chip. Tested against ROHS / CE / REACH, and the surface is wipe-clean, which matters in a shared training floor.不会。外壳是整体模压硅胶包裹铸铁芯，本身没有漆层可掉。通过 ROHS / CE / REACH 检测，表面可直接擦拭清洁 —— 公共训练区很重要。
Can we order the whole floor in our club colours?能按我们会所配色订整层设备吗？
Yes, and we recommend it. Send your two accent colours plus your logo; we apply them across mats, dumbbells, barbells, balls, rings, ropes, bands and rollers, then keep the recipe on file. Reorders match because the files never leave us.可以，而且我们建议这样做。给我们两个主色 + Logo，我们统一应用到地垫、哑铃、杠铃、配重球、配重环、跳绳、弹力带与泡沫轴上，并归档配方，之后补货完全一致。
What about lead time for a club-scale order?俱乐部级别的订单交期多久？
Sampling 7 days. Mass production is typically 20–30 days depending on mix, and we run 10 production lines with 1,000,000 mats/year capacity, so a mixed floor order does not have to wait for a single slow SKU.打样 7 天。量产一般 20–30 天，视组合而定。我们有 10 条产线、年产 100 万张垫的产能，整层混合订单不会被某一个慢款拖住。
