How to buy

How experienced buyers order this range

Gyms buy in three buckets: capital equipment at fit-out, consumables on an annual cycle (ropes, bands, mats) and retail stock at the front desk. The single biggest saving is ordering all three from one factory on one colour reference — no mismatch between the dumbbell rack and the mats, no three different lead times. We can also laser-engrave your club logo into the silicone weights and mats at no tooling cost.

FAQ

Gyms & Fitness Clubs — buyer questions

Do the silicone weights chip or peel with daily gym use?
No. The skin is a single moulded silicone shell over a cast-iron core — there is no paint layer to chip. The surface is wipe-clean, which matters in a shared training floor.
Can we order the whole floor in our club colours?
Yes, and we recommend it. Send your two accent colours plus your logo; we apply them across mats, dumbbells, barbells, balls, rings, ropes, bands and rollers, then keep the recipe on file. Reorders match because the files never leave us.
What about lead time for a club-scale order?
Sampling 7 days. Mass production is typically 20–30 days depending on mix, and we run 10 production lines with 1,000,000 mats/year capacity, so a mixed floor order does not have to wait for a single slow SKU.
