How to buy采购建议

How experienced buyers order this range有经验的买家是怎么下这单的

Buyers in this category should specify three numbers before anything else: density (durometer), diameter and surface pattern. Density decides whether the roller holds up under a 90 kg athlete; diameter decides how much of the muscle group it covers per stroke; pattern decides whether it is a general release tool or a trigger-point tool. Ask us for a density and pattern sample set — we send EVA and EPP in the same size so your clinicians can compare before you commit to a container.这个品类的采购方在下单前应先定三个数字：密度（硬度）、直径与表面纹理。密度决定这根轴能不能承受 90 kg 的运动员；直径决定单次滚压能覆盖多大肌群；纹理决定它是通用放松工具还是激痛点工具。可以找我们要密度与纹理的样品组 —— 同尺寸的 EVA 与 EPP 各一根，让你这边的治疗师比较过再决定整柜。

FAQ常见问题

Recovery & Physio — buyer questions恢复与理疗 —— 买家常问

What is the difference between EVA and EPP rollers?EVA 与 EPP 泡沫轴有什么区别？
EVA is softer and lighter — comfortable for general self-release and studio use. EPP is denser and springs back, so it does not compress under heavy athletes and is the usual clinic choice. We make both, in the same sizes, so you can compare like for like.EVA 更软更轻，适合日常自主放松与工作室使用。EPP 密度更高、回弹性好，在大体重运动员身下不塌陷，是诊所的常规选择。两种我们都做，尺寸一致，可直接对比。
Can we order custom textures and densities?能定制纹理与密度吗？
Yes — surface pattern, durometer, length, diameter and colour are all set per order. Tooling for a new texture is quoted once and then lives with your SKU, so reorders carry no tooling cost.可以 —— 表面纹理、硬度、长度、直径与颜色全部按单指定。新纹理的开模费只报一次，之后归属你的 SKU，补货不再收开模费。
Are the fascia guns suitable for professional clinic use?筋膜枪适合专业诊所使用吗？
The professional build has adjustable amplitude and speed with interchangeable heads, and is CE / ROHS compliant. We supply both the clinic version and the consumer retail version — tell us which channel you sell into and we will spec the right one.专业款可调振幅与转速，配可更换按摩头，符合 CE / ROHS。诊所版与消费零售版我们都供，说明你的销售渠道，我们会推荐合适的那一款。
