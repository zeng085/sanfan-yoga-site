From raw foam to finished mat, under our own roof

In-house from foaming to finishing. We operate three owned plants — two dedicated to TPE yoga mats and one to PU / rubber mats — running 6 foaming machines and 10 production lines. Because foaming, the most capacity-critical step, happens inside our own factories, we hold a steady annual output of about 1,000,000 mats and control quality from raw material through to the finished mat.
