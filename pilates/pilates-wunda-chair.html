OEM / ODM

What can be customized

  • Timber & finish — Solid timber, aluminium or steel; or we build to the material and finish spec your own brand already uses.
  • Upholstery — Microfibre, thickened PU, eco silicone leather or PU leather, with polyurethane foam. Any Pantone colour.
  • Springs — German Wagner springs, German piano-wire springs, Korean Sangbo steel or domestic carbon steel — set per model and resistance profile.
  • Hardware & rails — Stainless-steel rails, hardware and tube; silent frosted rollers with sealed bearings; 3-position spring bars.
  • Branding — Laser-engraved or branded plate on the frame, woven label on the upholstery, custom crate printing.
  • Packaging — Individual wooden crate (or carton for the foldable and aluminium lines), FBA-ready labelling on request.

FAQ

Pilates Wunda Chair — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity for a private-label run?
MOQ is quoted per model and finish, because frame timber, spring set and upholstery each change the run length. Tell us the model number and the quantity you have in mind and we return the exact minimum for that build — starting with a single model is normal, you do not have to take the whole line.
How fast can I get a sample?
A physical sample takes 7 working days, so you can sign off the geometry, timber finish and upholstery before committing. Specification sheets and photos covering all 9 models in this line are available immediately if you want to shortlist first.
What can be customized?
Your logo on the frame, your colour and upholstery spec, your own model numbering and spec sheet, and the geometry itself — Australian and European builds are both in production. Cartons and crates can carry your artwork, and we can work to a specification you already have.
Are the materials and compliance documents suitable for the US and EU?
Material is set per model — see the material column in the model table above for the code you are considering; we machine in solid timber, aluminium and steel. For OEM and ODM programmes we build to the material and finish specification your own brand already uses. Rails, springs and bearings are specified for studio duty. The factory is ISO 9001:2015 certified and was amfori BSCI audited in 2025 (renewal in progress); ask us which test reports are available for the model you are considering.
How is it packed and shipped?
Complete apparatus ships in an individual wooden crate; foldable and aluminium models travel in cartons. We quote FOB, EXW or DDP, can label cartons for Amazon FBA, and a single container can mix models from this line, so a first order can be spread across your best-selling pieces.
How much floor space does a Wunda chair need?
It is the smallest piece in the classical set — roughly the footprint of a dining chair plus clearance for the pedal to swing. That is why it is the usual first purchase for a small studio or a home practice room, and why clinics pair it with a reformer rather than starting from a Cadillac.
