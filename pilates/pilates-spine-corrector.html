OEM / ODM

What can be customized可以定制什么

  • Timber & finish — Domestic or imported rubberwood, FAS-grade white maple, piano lacquer, Croatian beech, aluminium or 316 steel.
  • Upholstery — Microfibre, thickened PU, eco silicone leather or PU leather, with polyurethane foam. Any Pantone colour.
  • Springs — German Wagner springs, German piano-wire springs, Korean Sangbo steel or domestic carbon steel — set per model and resistance profile.
  • Hardware & rails — Stainless-steel rails, hardware and tube; silent frosted rollers with sealed bearings; 3-position spring bars.
  • Branding — Laser-engraved or branded plate on the frame, woven label on the upholstery, custom crate printing.
  • Packaging — Individual wooden crate (or carton for the foldable and aluminium lines), FBA-ready labelling on request.
  • 木种与表面 — 国产或进口橡胶木、FAS 级白枫木、钢琴烤漆、克罗地亚榉木、铝合金或 316 钢。
  • 皮革与坐垫 — 超纤皮革、加厚 PU、生态硅胶皮革或 PU 皮革，配聚氨酯海绵。颜色可按潘通色卡定制。
  • 弹簧 — 德国瓦格纳弹簧、德国琴钢丝弹簧、韩国象牌钢或国产碳钢弹簧，按型号与阻力谱配比。
  • 五金与轨道 — 不锈钢轨道、五金件与钢管；静音磨砂滑轮配内置轴承；三档挂簧板。
  • 品牌要素 — 床架可激光雕刻或做品牌铭牌，坐垫可缝织标，木箱可印定制标识。
  • 包装 — 整机木箱独立包装（折叠款与铝合金款走纸箱），可按需做 FBA 标签。

FAQ常见问题

Pilates Spine Corrector — buyer questions普拉提矫正器 —— 买家常问

What is the minimum order quantity for a private-label run?私标订单的最小起订量是多少？
MOQ is quoted per model and finish, because frame timber, spring set and upholstery each change the run length. Tell us the model number and the quantity you have in mind and we return the exact minimum for that build — starting with a single model is normal, you do not have to take the whole line.起订量按型号与工艺单独核算 —— 木料、弹簧组、皮革包覆各自都会改变单次生产批量。告诉我们型号与意向数量，我们给出该配置的准确起订量；从单一型号起步是常态，不必整条产品线一起下单。
How fast can I get a sample?样品多久能拿到？
A physical sample takes 7 working days, so you can sign off the geometry, timber finish and upholstery before committing. Specification sheets and photos covering all 3 models in this line are available immediately if you want to shortlist first.实物样品 7 个工作日交付，便于在下单前确认结构尺寸、木作工艺与皮革包覆。若想先筛选，本线全部 3 个型号的规格书与实拍图可立即提供。
What can be customized?可以定制哪些内容？
Your logo on the frame, your colour and upholstery spec, your own model numbering and spec sheet, and the geometry itself — Australian and European builds are both in production. Cartons and crates can carry your artwork, and we can work to a specification you already have.床架打您的 Logo、按您的配色与皮革规格、用您的型号编号与规格书，结构本身也可调整 —— 澳式与欧式两种结构均在产。纸箱与木箱可印您的品牌画面，也可按您现有的规格书执行。
Are the materials and compliance documents suitable for the US and EU?材料与合规文件能满足美国与欧盟要求吗？
Frames are built in imported rubberwood, FAS-grade white maple, piano-lacquer white maple and Croatian beech, with rails, springs and bearings specified for studio duty. Products are tested against REACH, CA Prop 65 and 6P/7P, and the factory is ISO 9001 and BSCI audited. Test reports ship with the order.床架采用进口橡胶木、FAS 级白枫木、钢琴漆白枫木与克罗地亚榉木，导轨、弹簧与轴承按场馆级使用选型。产品按 REACH、加州 65 号提案与 6P/7P 送检，工厂通过 ISO 9001 与 BSCI 审核，检测报告随货提供。
How is it packed and shipped?包装与运输怎么走？
Complete apparatus ships in an individual wooden crate; foldable and aluminium models travel in cartons. We quote FOB, EXW or DDP, can label cartons for Amazon FBA, and a single container can mix models from this line, so a first order can be spread across your best-selling pieces.整机木箱独立包装，折叠款与铝合金款走纸箱。可按 FOB / EXW / DDP 报价，可按需贴亚马逊 FBA 标签；一个柜子可以混装本线的多个型号，首单可以铺在您最好卖的几款上。
Is the spine corrector suitable for a home studio?矫正器适合家用工作室吗？
Yes — it is a mat and a barrel in one, takes about the space of a large yoga mat, and needs no springs or frame assembly. Studios buy it alongside a reformer; home and small-class buyers often start with it alone because it covers much of the mat repertoire.适合 —— 它等于垫上器械与桶的结合，占地约一张大瑜伽垫，不需要弹簧，也不需要拼装框架。场馆通常与核心床一起采购；家庭与小班客户常从它单独起步，因为垫上动作库的大部分都能覆盖。
