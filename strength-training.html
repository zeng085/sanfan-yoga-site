Strength and Fitness Guide

Silicone-skinned weights and the case for one colour system

Cast iron is the core of every weight on this page, but the skin is what the customer pays for. A silicone coating deadens the noise when two pieces meet, protects wooden floors and studio walls, and feels warm rather than cold in the hand. That is why it has displaced dipped and painted iron in studio and boutique-gym fit-outs. Underneath, the same casting gives the same density and the same balance.

One colour system is the commercial argument. Dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, weighted balls and rings can all be produced in the same palette, in six silicone colourways for the smaller formats. A gym that buys one coordinated set does not come back for a mismatched second order, and a retail buyer gets a shelf that reads as a single brand rather than a mixed pile.

The light end of the range covers abs and cardio: ab rollers, a multi-function board at 106 × 41 × 7 cm, a compact training board, suspension kits, and hydraulic steppers. Weights run across silicone dumbbells, cast-iron dumbbells in a range of loads, and bars at 91 cm and 110 cm. Power supply and controls on the electronic items are confirmed per order, so tell us the destination market. MOQ is 500 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.

FAQ

Strength equipment questions buyers ask

What is the difference between a silicone skin and dipped iron?
A silicone skin is a thicker, softer coating. It muffles the clang when two pieces meet, it does not chip onto the floor the way a painted or dipped finish can, and it does not feel cold in the hand. The casting underneath is the same, so weight and balance are unchanged.
Are the dumbbells adjustable?
Both options exist. Fixed cast-iron dumbbells run across a range of loads in the standard increments, and an adjustable build takes plates so one pair covers several loads. Tell us which one your customer expects and we will quote that.
Can the whole range share one colour?
Yes — that is what the range is built around. Six silicone colourways are available across the smaller formats, and bars, rings, kettlebells and balls can all be produced in the same palette, so a gym fit-out arrives coordinated.
How are the steppers and training boards powered?
The hydraulic steppers are self-contained and need no power. The electronic training boards run on a cell or a rechargeable pack, and the exact supply depends on the market you ship into. Confirm the destination and we will quote the compliant version.
What is the MOQ and the sampling time?
MOQ is 500 pcs per item and colour, with sampling in about 7 days. Mixed orders that combine a board, a pair of dumbbells and a stepper are standard as long as each item meets its own minimum.
