Silicone-skinned weights and the case for one colour system硅胶包覆配重，以及统一配色为什么值钱

Cast iron is the core of every weight on this page, but the skin is what the customer pays for. A silicone coating deadens the noise when two pieces meet, protects wooden floors and studio walls, and feels warm rather than cold in the hand. That is why it has displaced dipped and painted iron in studio and boutique-gym fit-outs. Underneath, the same casting gives the same density and the same balance.这一页每一件配重的芯都是铸铁，但客户真正付钱买的是那层皮。硅胶包覆能在两件器材相碰时把声音闷住，保护木地板与墙面，握在手里是温的而不是冰的。这就是它在工作室与小而精的健身房采购里取代浸塑与喷漆铸铁的原因。而底下同一套铸造，密度与配重平衡完全一样。

One colour system is the commercial argument. Dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, weighted balls and rings can all be produced in the same palette, in six silicone colourways for the smaller formats. A gym that buys one coordinated set does not come back for a mismatched second order, and a retail buyer gets a shelf that reads as a single brand rather than a mixed pile.统一配色是这里的商业理由。哑铃、杠铃、壶铃、负重球与训练环都能做成同一套色系，小件有六种硅胶配色可选。健身房买了一套配套的，就不会再回头补一个颜色对不上的第二批；零售买家拿到的货架，看起来是一个品牌，而不是一堆杂牌。

The light end of the range covers abs and cardio: ab rollers, a multi-function board at 106 × 41 × 7 cm, a compact training board, suspension kits, and hydraulic steppers. Weights run across silicone dumbbells, cast-iron dumbbells in a range of loads, and bars at 91 cm and 110 cm. Power supply and controls on the electronic items are confirmed per order, so tell us the destination market. MOQ is 100 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.轻器械一侧覆盖腹部与有氧：健腹轮、106 × 41 × 7 cm 的多功能健身板、紧凑型训练板、悬吊训练套装与液压登山机。配重这边有硅胶哑铃、多档位铸铁哑铃，以及 91 cm 与 110 cm 两种杠铃杆。电子类产品的供电与控制方式按订单确认，所以请告诉我们目标市场。100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Strength equipment questions buyers ask买家会问的力量器械问题

What is the difference between a silicone skin and dipped iron?硅胶包覆和浸塑铸铁差在哪？
A silicone skin is a thicker, softer coating. It muffles the clang when two pieces meet, it does not chip onto the floor the way a painted or dipped finish can, and it does not feel cold in the hand. The casting underneath is the same, so weight and balance are unchanged.硅胶包覆是更厚、更软的涂层。它能把两件相碰时的响声闷住；不像喷漆或浸塑那样会掉皮掉到地板上；握起来也不冰手。底下的铸件完全一样，所以重量与配重平衡不变。
Are the dumbbells adjustable?哑铃是可调的吗？
Both options exist. Fixed cast-iron dumbbells run across a range of loads in the standard increments, and an adjustable build takes plates so one pair covers several loads. Tell us which one your customer expects and we will quote that.两种都有。固定式铸铁哑铃覆盖常规档位的多个重量；可调款能加减配重片，一对顶好几对。告诉我们你的客户期待哪一种，我们就报哪一种。
Can the whole range share one colour?全系列能统一配色吗？
Yes — that is what the range is built around. Six silicone colourways are available across the smaller formats, and bars, rings, kettlebells and balls can all be produced in the same palette, so a gym fit-out arrives coordinated.可以 —— 这条线就是围着这件事搭的。小件有六种硅胶配色，杠铃、训练环、壶铃与负重球都能做成同一套色，所以健身房一次配齐，到货就是配套的。
How are the steppers and training boards powered?登山机与训练板的供电方式是什么？
The hydraulic steppers are self-contained and need no power. The electronic training boards run on a cell or a rechargeable pack, and the exact supply depends on the market you ship into. Confirm the destination and we will quote the compliant version.液压登山机自带阻力，不需要供电。电子训练板用电池或充电电池组，具体用哪种取决于你的目的市场。确认销往哪里，我们报的就是能合规进口的版本。
What is the MOQ and the sampling time?起订量和打样时间是多少？
MOQ is 100 pcs per item and colour, with sampling in about 7 days. Mixed orders that combine a board, a pair of dumbbells and a stepper are standard as long as each item meets its own minimum.每个单品、每个颜色 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。把训练板、一对哑铃和登山机并成一张单很常规，只要每项各满起订量。
