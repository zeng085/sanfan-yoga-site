Resistance Band Guide

Resistance bands: material, shape and tension

Three materials cover almost every brief. TPE has no latex proteins and no rubber smell, which is what sells into clinics, schools and any market where a latex allergy has to be designed out. Natural latex stretches further and costs less per band, so it is still the choice for heavy resistance work. Silicone keeps its colour and does not go tacky, which suits retail sets that have to look presentable after a year on a shelf.

Shape decides the exercise. Loop bands go round the thighs and knees for warm-ups and glute work. Tubes with handles take a foot pedal or a door anchor for pulling movements. Figure-8 bands fit in a pocket and isolate one arm or one leg. Power bands are the heavy, long-format tool for assisted pull-ups and squats. Each format is cut in its own standard size, from a 60 cm loop up to a 150 cm standing set.

Tension is graded rather than guessed: every format is produced in light, medium and heavy, and the grade is coded by colour so a customer can read it without a chart. Sets, printed logos and retail packaging all come out of one production run, and colours can be matched across the whole set so it photographs as a kit. MOQ is 1,000 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.

FAQ

Resistance band questions buyers ask

Should we use TPE or natural latex?
TPE if the product has to be latex-free — it has no latex proteins and no rubber smell, which clears it for clinics, schools and allergy-aware retail. Natural latex if you need maximum stretch at the lowest cost per band. Silicone sits between the two and keeps its appearance longest in a retail set.
How is the resistance graded?
Every format is produced in light, medium and heavy, and the grade is coded by colour so a customer can read it without a chart. Loop bands are thickness-graded at 0.8 mm, 1.0 mm and 1.2 mm; the standing tube sets are width-graded at 0.35 mm, 0.45 mm and 0.55 mm.
Which shape suits which customer?
Loops for classes, physio and warm-ups. Tubes with handles for home gyms, because they anchor to a door or a foot. Figure-8 for travel and single-limb work. Power bands for assisted pull-ups and squats, where the load is the point.
Can you build a retail set with our branding?
Yes. A set of three or five grades in a printed pouch or box, colour-matched, with your logo on the band and on the packaging, is one production run. Send the layout and we will confirm what prints cleanly on an elastic surface before you commit.
What is the MOQ?
MOQ is 1,000 pcs per colour and grade, with sampling in about 7 days. Mixed sets count per grade, so a five-band kit with 1,000 pcs of each grade is a standard order.
