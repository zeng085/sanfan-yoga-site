Resistance bands: material, shape and tension弹力带：材质、形态与阻力

Three materials cover almost every brief. TPE has no latex proteins and no rubber smell, which is what sells into clinics, schools and any market where a latex allergy has to be designed out. Natural latex stretches further and costs less per band, so it is still the choice for heavy resistance work. Silicone keeps its colour and does not go tacky, which suits retail sets that have to look presentable after a year on a shelf.三种材质几乎能覆盖所有需求。TPE 不含乳胶蛋白、没有胶味，适合诊所、学校，以及任何必须回避乳胶过敏的市场；天然乳胶拉伸幅度更大、单条成本更低，大阻力训练还是选它；硅胶不褪色、不发粘，适合要在货架上放一年还要好看的零售套装。

Shape decides the exercise. Loop bands go round the thighs and knees for warm-ups and glute work. Tubes with handles take a foot pedal or a door anchor for pulling movements. Figure-8 bands fit in a pocket and isolate one arm or one leg. Power bands are the heavy, long-format tool for assisted pull-ups and squats. Each format is cut in its own standard size, from a 60 cm loop up to a 150 cm standing set.形态决定练什么。环形带绕在大腿和膝盖上做热身与臀腿；带把手的拉力管接脚踏或门扣，做各种拉的动作；8 字带能塞进口袋，单独练一侧手臂或腿；力量带是重型长款，做助力引体与深蹲。每种形态各有自己的标准尺寸，从 60 cm 的环形带到 150 cm 的站姿套装。

Tension is graded rather than guessed: every format is produced in light, medium and heavy, and the grade is coded by colour so a customer can read it without a chart. Sets, printed logos and retail packaging all come out of one production run, and colours can be matched across the whole set so it photographs as a kit. MOQ is 100 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.阻力是分级做出来的，不是猜的：每种形态都分轻、中、重三档，用颜色区分，客户不用看对照表就认得出。套装、印刷 Logo 与零售包装一次生产完成，整套配色可以统一，拍出来是一整套。100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Resistance band questions buyers ask买家会问的弹力带问题

Should we use TPE or natural latex?用 TPE 还是天然乳胶？
TPE if the product has to be latex-free — it has no latex proteins and no rubber smell, which clears it for clinics, schools and allergy-aware retail. Natural latex if you need maximum stretch at the lowest cost per band. Silicone sits between the two and keeps its appearance longest in a retail set.产品必须无乳胶就用 TPE —— 它不含乳胶蛋白、没有胶味，诊所、学校和在意过敏问题的零售都能上；需要最大拉伸幅度、单条成本最低就选天然乳胶；硅胶介于两者之间，在零售套装里外观保持得最久。
How is the resistance graded?阻力怎么分级？
Every format is produced in light, medium and heavy, and the grade is coded by colour so a customer can read it without a chart. Loop bands are thickness-graded at 0.8 mm, 1.0 mm and 1.2 mm; the standing tube sets are width-graded at 0.35 mm, 0.45 mm and 0.55 mm.每种形态都做轻、中、重三档，用颜色区分档位，客户不用看对照表就认得。环形带按厚度分级：0.8 mm、1.0 mm、1.2 mm；站姿拉力管套装按宽度分级：0.35 mm、0.45 mm、0.55 mm。
Which shape suits which customer?哪种形态适合哪类客户？
Loops for classes, physio and warm-ups. Tubes with handles for home gyms, because they anchor to a door or a foot. Figure-8 for travel and single-limb work. Power bands for assisted pull-ups and squats, where the load is the point.环形带用于团课、康复与热身；带把手的拉力管用于家庭健身，可以固定在门上或踩在脚下；8 字带用于出差与单侧训练；力量带用于助力引体与深蹲这类以负重为目的的动作。
Can you build a retail set with our branding?能做带我们品牌的零售套装吗？
Yes. A set of three or five grades in a printed pouch or box, colour-matched, with your logo on the band and on the packaging, is one production run. Send the layout and we will confirm what prints cleanly on an elastic surface before you commit.可以。三档或五档的套装，装进印刷袋或印刷盒，整套配色统一，带身和包装上都印你的 Logo，一次生产完成。把版面发来，我们会先告诉你哪些内容在弹性表面上印出来是干净的，再让你决定。
What is the MOQ?起订量是多少？
MOQ is 100 pcs per colour and grade, with sampling in about 7 days. Mixed sets count per grade, so a five-band kit with 100 pcs of each grade is a standard order.每个颜色、每个档位 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。混装套装按档位分别计数，所以五档套装、每档 100 片是一张很常规的订单。
