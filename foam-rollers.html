Choosing the right foam roller for your range怎么给你的产品线挑泡沫轴

EVA and EPP are the two foams most rollers are made from, and they suit different customers. EVA is denser and feels firmer under load, which is what a physio or a serious lifter wants for deep-tissue work. EPP is lighter, springs back faster and holds its shape after repeated compression, so it carries high-turnover gym and studio orders. We run both on the same line, so you can sample the two side by side before committing to a range.泡沫轴最常用的是 EVA 与 EPP 两种发泡材质，面向的客户也不一样。EVA 密度更高、承压更硬，物理治疗师或重度训练者做深层放松就认这个手感；EPP 更轻、回弹更快，反复压过还能保持形状，适合走量快的健身房与瑜伽馆订单。两条线我们都在跑，你可以两种都打样，对比过再定产品线。

Structure matters as much as material. Solid rollers are the default for deep-tissue compression. Hollow cores cut weight and freight on the long 45 cm (18") format, which is the size most export orders ask for. A vibrating build adds a rechargeable motor for recovery studios and sports teams, and wolf-tooth or grid textures sit on top of any of them when a customer wants a firmer, more targeted surface. Our EVA rollers run at 30 cm, 33 cm and 45 cm, all on a 12.5 cm diameter.结构与材质同样重要。实心款是深层按压的默认选择；长款 45 cm（18"）做空心能压重量、省运费，而这个尺寸正是出口订单问得最多的。震动款加了可充电电机，面向恢复工作室与运动队；狼牙纹或浮点纹则可以在任意结构上再叠一层更硬、更聚焦的表面。我们的 EVA 泡沫轴有 30 cm、33 cm、45 cm 三种长度，全系直径 12.5 cm。

Everything on the spec sheet is set per order: density and hardness, length, colour, logo and packaging. Colourways can be matched to the mats on the same shipment, so the range reads as one collection instead of a pile of odds and ends. Tell us the density you want to hit and we will sample it — MOQ is 100 pcs and sampling runs about 7 days.规格表上每一项都按订单定：密度与软硬、长度、颜色、Logo 与包装。配色可以和同一批的垫子对上，让整条线看起来像一个系列，而不是一堆杂货。把你想要达到的密度告诉我们，我们打样给你 —— 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Foam roller questions buyers ask买家会问的泡沫轴问题

What is the difference between EVA and EPP foam rollers?EVA 和 EPP 泡沫轴有什么区别？
EVA is denser and firmer, so it holds up under deep-tissue pressure and feels solid in the hand. EPP is lighter, rebounds faster and keeps its shape after repeated compression, which suits high-volume gym and studio orders. Both compounds run on the same line, so density and hardness are set to your brief either way.EVA 密度更高、更硬，扛得住深层按压，握在手里也更实。EPP 更轻、回弹更快，反复压过还能保持形状，适合走量大的健身房与瑜伽馆订单。两种材质同一条线生产，密度与软硬都能按你的要求调。
Which lengths and diameters can you run?你们能做哪些长度和直径？
Our EVA rollers run at 30 cm, 33 cm and 45 cm, all on a 12.5 cm diameter, and the 45 cm (18") format is the one most export orders ask for. Longer formats and non-standard diameters come off the same tooling family, so they need a quotation but no new mould.我们的 EVA 泡沫轴有 30 cm、33 cm、45 cm 三种长度，直径统一 12.5 cm，其中 45 cm（18"）是出口订单问得最多的规格。更长的尺寸与非标直径与常规款共用同一套模具，所以只需要报价，不需要重新开模。
How does the vibrating build work, and how is it charged?震动款是怎么工作的？怎么充电？
A rechargeable motor sits inside the core and is switched on at the end cap. Run time, charge time and battery certification are confirmed per order, because different markets accept different cells. Tell us the destination market and we quote the version that clears import there.可充电电机装在轴芯内部，在端盖处开关。续航时间、充电时长与电池认证都按订单确认 —— 不同市场接受的电池不一样。告诉我们目标市场，我们报的是能通过那边进口的版本。
Can we change the density and hardness?密度和软硬能改吗？
Yes. Density and hardness are set at the foaming stage rather than picked from a catalogue, so a firmer roller for clinical work or a softer one for beginners is a production parameter, not a different product. Send a sample of what you sell today and we will match it.可以。密度与软硬在发泡环节就定下来，不是从目录里挑现成的，所以临床用的硬款或新手用的软款只是一个生产参数，不是两个产品。把你现在在卖的样品寄来，我们照着做。
What is the MOQ, and can the colours match our mats?起订量多少？颜色能和我们的垫子对上吗？
MOQ is 100 pcs per colour and design, with sampling in about 7 days. Colourways can be matched to the mats on the same shipment, and the logo plus retail packaging are handled in the same production run.起订量 100 片／颜色与款式，约 7 天出样。配色可以和同一批的垫子对上，Logo 与零售包装都在同一次生产里做完。
