Foam Roller Guide

Choosing the right foam roller for your range

EVA and EPP are the two foams most rollers are made from, and they suit different customers. EVA is denser and feels firmer under load, which is what a physio or a serious lifter wants for deep-tissue work. EPP is lighter, springs back faster and holds its shape after repeated compression, so it carries high-turnover gym and studio orders. We run both on the same line, so you can sample the two side by side before committing to a range.

Structure matters as much as material. Solid rollers are the default for deep-tissue compression. Hollow cores cut weight and freight on the long 45 cm (18") format, which is the size most export orders ask for. A vibrating build adds a rechargeable motor for recovery studios and sports teams, and wolf-tooth or grid textures sit on top of any of them when a customer wants a firmer, more targeted surface. Our EVA rollers run at 30 cm, 33 cm and 45 cm, all on a 12.5 cm diameter.

Everything on the spec sheet is set per order: density and hardness, length, colour, logo and packaging. Colourways can be matched to the mats on the same shipment, so the range reads as one collection instead of a pile of odds and ends. Tell us the density you want to hit and we will sample it — MOQ is 500 pcs and sampling runs about 7 days.

FAQ

Foam roller questions buyers ask

What is the difference between EVA and EPP foam rollers?
EVA is denser and firmer, so it holds up under deep-tissue pressure and feels solid in the hand. EPP is lighter, rebounds faster and keeps its shape after repeated compression, which suits high-volume gym and studio orders. Both compounds run on the same line, so density and hardness are set to your brief either way.
Which lengths and diameters can you run?
Our EVA rollers run at 30 cm, 33 cm and 45 cm, all on a 12.5 cm diameter, and the 45 cm (18") format is the one most export orders ask for. Longer formats and non-standard diameters come off the same tooling family, so they need a quotation but no new mould.
How does the vibrating build work, and how is it charged?
A rechargeable motor sits inside the core and is switched on at the end cap. Run time, charge time and battery certification are confirmed per order, because different markets accept different cells. Tell us the destination market and we quote the version that clears import there.
Can we change the density and hardness?
Yes. Density and hardness are set at the foaming stage rather than picked from a catalogue, so a firmer roller for clinical work or a softer one for beginners is a production parameter, not a different product. Send a sample of what you sell today and we will match it.
What is the MOQ, and can the colours match our mats?
MOQ is 500 pcs per colour and design, with sampling in about 7 days. Colourways can be matched to the mats on the same shipment, and the logo plus retail packaging are handled in the same production run.
