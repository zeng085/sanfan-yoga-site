Suede and Cork Guide麂皮与软木选型

Suede or cork: two surfaces, two stories麂皮还是软木：两种面层，两个卖点

Suede microfibre and natural cork are the two surfaces behind almost every design-led mat on the market, and they behave differently. Suede feels dry and soft, takes a high-definition print with real depth of colour, and grips harder as it gets damp — which is why heated studios specify it. Cork feels firmer and more grounded underfoot and sells on the material story rather than on the artwork.麂皮（超细纤维）与天然软木是市面上几乎所有设计驱动型垫子背后的两种面层，手感差异很大。麂皮干爽柔软，能印出有色彩深度的高清图案，而且越湿越防滑 —— 高温教室点名要它就是这个原因。软木踩上去更实、更稳，卖的是材质本身，不是图案。

The surface is only half the specification, because either one bonds to either base. Suede over natural rubber is the premium studio build, and suede over TPE gives the same print quality at a lighter weight and a lower landed cost. Cork pairs with rubber for the firmest feel, and cork over TPE brings the weight down for folding and travel formats.面层只是规格的一半，因为这两种面层都能贴两种底。麂皮配天然橡胶是高端馆用结构；麂皮配 TPE 印花品质一样，但更轻、到岸成本更低。软木配橡胶踩感最实，软木配 TPE 则把重量压下来，适合折叠款与旅行款。

Prints are where this family earns its margin: gradients, botanical and geometric artwork, florals, and licensed or collaborative designs, reproduced across the full face. Foldable builds pack into a smaller box without the mat curling at the edges, which is what mail-order and travel customers ask for. Send the artwork and we will sample it — MOQ is 100 pcs, samples in about 7 days.印花是这一族赚利润的地方：渐变、植物、几何、花卉，以及联名或授权图案，都能满版印制。折叠款能装进更小的箱子，垫子边缘也不会卷起来，这是邮购与旅行客户点名要的。把设计稿发来我们打样 —— 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Suede and cork mat questions buyers ask买家会问的麂皮与软木垫问题

Suede or cork — how do we choose?麂皮还是软木，怎么选？
Suede if the range is sold on artwork and colour, because it holds a print far better and gets grippier as it gets damp. Cork if the range is sold on material and sustainability, because the surface itself is the story. Many customers run both: cork as the anchor line and suede for the seasonal prints.靠图案和颜色卖的系列选麂皮 —— 它印花表现好得多，而且越湿越防滑；靠材质与环保概念卖的系列选软木 —— 面层本身就是故事。很多客户两条都做：软木当基石款，麂皮做季节性印花款。
Why do these surfaces grip better when wet?为什么这两种面层遇汗更防滑？
Microfibre suede absorbs moisture into the fibre and the surface tension rises, so the hand holds harder as the mat gets damp. Cork does the same through its own cellular structure. It is the reason both are specified for heated and dynamic classes.超细纤维麂皮把水分吸进纤维里，表面张力上升，所以越湿手按得越牢；软木靠自身的细胞结构达到同样的效果。这就是高温课与动态课都指定这两种面层的原因。
Which base layers can we pair with them?可以配哪几种底？
Natural rubber for the firmest, most grounded build, or TPE for a lighter mat at a lower landed cost. Both surfaces bond to both bases, which is where the combinations come from. Tell us the retail price band and we will recommend one instead of quoting everything.天然橡胶做踩感最实的结构，TPE 做更轻、到岸成本更低的版本。两种面层都能贴这两种底，组合就是这么来的。把零售价带告诉我们，我们只推荐一种，不会全部报一遍。
Can the mat carry our own artwork?能印我们自己的图案吗？
Yes — that is what this family is for. Gradients, botanical, geometric and floral prints all reproduce on suede, across the full face. Send the file and we will confirm how it samples before you commit to a production run.能 —— 这一族就是为这个存在的。渐变、植物、几何与花卉图案在麂皮上都能表现，而且是满版印。把文件发来，我们会先把打样效果确认给你，再让你决定要不要量产。
How should these mats be cared for?平时怎么保养？
Wipe with water and a pH-neutral cleaner, dry flat and away from direct sun, and never put suede or cork through a machine. Cork can be refreshed with a dilute vinegar wipe. Looked after that way, both surfaces hold up for years of studio use.用清水加 pH 中性清洁剂擦拭，平摊阴干、避开直晒，麂皮与软木都绝对不能机洗。软木可以用稀释的白醋擦一遍焕新。这样保养，两种面层都能扛住多年馆用。
