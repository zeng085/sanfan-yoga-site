Yoga Mat Factory Capabilities: Equipment, Processes & Audit Checklist

Updated 2026-11-07 · Published by FUJIAN SANFAN Sports Products Co., Ltd. (Jinjiang, Fujian, China)

Foaming and sheet making

Foaming is the capacity-critical step in mat production. SANFAN runs 6 foaming machines across its plants. Sheets are then sliced to thickness, embossed and stocked before finishing.

TPE line

Two of SANFAN’s three plants are dedicated to TPE yoga mats. The TPE line covers mixing, extrusion, foaming, calendering and cutting in-house.

We have not yet published labelled footage of the TPE extrusion and calendering steps. Ask for a live video walkthrough of the TPE plant and we will show the running line.

Natural rubber line

The natural rubber line runs internal-mixer (Banbury) mixing, open-mill work, calendering, a continuous foaming line, vulcanization and cutting in-house.

PU + natural rubber composite line

PU/rubber mats combine a PU top layer with a natural rubber base. SANFAN processes both layers and laminates them in-house by hot press or adhesive, followed by surface treatment.

Finishing: printing, engraving, cutting and packing

Finishing is done in-house: screen printing, digital/UV printing, suede heat-transfer printing, laser engraving, automatic cutting, roll packing and OEM packaging.

FAQ

Is SANFAN a factory or a trading company?

SANFAN is a manufacturer. FUJIAN SANFAN was founded in 2013 in Jinjiang, Fujian, operates three owned plants (two TPE, one PU/rubber) and runs foaming, calendering, lamination, printing, cutting and packing in-house.

Which yoga mat processes does SANFAN do in-house?

TPE mixing, extrusion, foaming, calendering and cutting; natural rubber mixing (internal mixer and open mill), calendering, continuous foaming, vulcanization and cutting; PU top-layer processing, rubber base processing and hot-press or adhesive lamination; plus screen printing, digital/UV printing, laser engraving, automatic cutting, roll packing and OEM packaging.

Which certificates and test reports does SANFAN hold?

ISO 9001:2015 (Hangzhou Beide, valid to June 2028); RoHS 2.0 test reports for TPE, PU rubber and suede rubber mats (August 2025); a California Prop 65 test report for a printed PU/rubber mat (BACL, April 2026); an amfori BSCI audit by SGS (June 2025, renewal in progress). Full reports are shared per material on request.

Can SANFAN provide REACH or SVHC test reports?

REACH/SVHC testing can be arranged on request for a specific order at a third-party lab. SANFAN does not claim an existing REACH report on this page.

Can I audit SANFAN before ordering?

Yes. You can request a live video-call walkthrough of the lines, a pre-production sample (about 7 days), the full test reports for your material, or an on-site visit to the Jinjiang factory. Write to sales@fjsanfan.com.

What is the minimum order quantity?

TPE mats from 100 pcs per design per colour; PU/rubber bulk from 300 pcs; printed PU from 1 pc; foam rollers 500 pcs; resistance bands 1,000 pcs. Prices and lead times are confirmed at quote.
