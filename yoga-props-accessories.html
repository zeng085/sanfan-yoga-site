Props and Accessories Guide

The add-on range that lifts every mat order

Props are the cheapest way to raise the value of a container that is already going out. A mat order is one line item; the same shipment with blocks, a ball, a ring and a bag behind it is a programme, at a fraction of the freight cost per unit. That is why this range is built to sit alongside the mat lines rather than compete with them.

Six groups do the work here. EVA blocks give support in restorative poses, and high-density blocks hold their shape instead of crushing under weight. Yoga balls run from a 25 cm (10") mini for pilates and rehab up to full-size anti-burst builds. Pilates rings come as a 34 cm silicone and fibreglass build or a 40 cm foam-padded version. Aerial hammocks run from 1 × 2.6 m to 5 × 2.8 m. Stretch boards lock the foot for calf and hamstring work, and carry bags finish the set.

Everything here is priced to ship with the mats, in the same colours. Blocks, rings and bags can be matched to the mat body or to the print, so a retail buyer receives a coordinated range instead of loose accessories. Custom colours and logos are set per order across the whole list. MOQ is 500 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.

FAQ

Yoga prop questions buyers ask

Can props be added to a mat order?
Yes, and that is what the range is for. Blocks, balls, rings, boards and bags are produced on their own lines but ship in the same container as the mats, so a buyer gets a coordinated programme and pays freight once. Each item still carries its own 500 pcs minimum.
How are the aerial hammocks installed, and what do they hold?
They hang from a ceiling or a frame using a mount kit, which we can supply with the hammock. Weight ratings depend on the fabric and the stitching pattern, so we confirm the rating for the specific build you order rather than quoting one figure across the range.
Can the block density be specified?
Yes. EVA blocks are produced at the density your brief calls for — a firmer block for weight-bearing support, a softer one for comfort in restorative work. Send a sample of what you stock now and we will match the feel.
Which yoga ball sizes do you make?
The mini ball is 25 cm (10") across, which is the size pilates and rehab customers use for core and balance work. Full-size anti-burst balls come in the standard diameters, and custom colours and print are set per order.
What is the MOQ across a mixed prop order?
MOQ is 500 pcs per item, with sampling in about 7 days. A mixed order can combine blocks, balls, rings and bags as long as each item reaches its own minimum, and the colours can be matched across the whole shipment.
