Building a recovery range that pairs with every mat order搭一条能和每张垫子配对的恢复线

Recovery tools are not interchangeable, and the difference is how much force the customer wants to put in. Fascia guns deliver high-frequency percussion through a motor, so the tool does the work. Fascia balls and rollers are body-weight tools — the user leans into them, which makes them cheap, silent and hard to break. Massage sticks reach the back and calves where your own hands cannot, and leg clamps compress the calf with no effort from the user at all.恢复工具之间不能互相替代，差别在于客户愿意自己使多大劲。筋膜枪靠电机输出高频振动，是工具在出力；筋膜球与泡沫轴是自重工具 —— 使用者把自己的体重压上去，所以便宜、安静、还不容易坏；按摩棒用来够自己的手够不到的背和小腿；腿部夹则完全不用使用者出力就能挤压小腿。

The gun range covers four grip types, because hand size and target area decide the sale: a compact white build for general retail, a T-shape for reaching the back, a ring-grip version that can be held two ways, and a professional model for therapists who run it all day. All four share the same motor family, so the difference is ergonomics rather than performance.筋膜枪有四种握型，因为手的大小和要打的部位决定了客户买哪一支：白色紧凑款走大众零售，T 型用来够背部，环形握把可以两种握法，专业款给整天不离手的理疗师。四种共用同一套电机，所以差别在人体工学，不在性能。

This is the category that pairs most naturally with a mat order, because recovery is what happens the day after the class. A gym or a studio buying mats can take guns, balls and sticks on the same shipment, in the same colours, and hand out or sell a complete recovery kit. Fascia balls are produced as a six-colour set for exactly that purpose. MOQ is 100 pcs, sampling in about 7 days.这一类和垫子订单配对最自然，因为恢复就是上完课第二天的事。买垫子的健身房或馆，可以在同一批里带上筋膜枪、筋膜球和按摩棒，配色统一，成套作为恢复包发放或销售。筋膜球专门做了六色套装就是这个用途。100 片起订，约 7 天出样。

FAQ常见问题

Massage and recovery questions buyers ask买家会问的按摩与恢复问题

What is the difference between the fascia gun models?几种筋膜枪差在哪？
They share a motor family; the difference is the grip. The white build is a compact all-rounder, the T-shape reaches the back and shoulders, the ring grip can be held two ways for different angles, and the professional model is built for all-day therapist use. Pick by who is holding it and where they need to reach.它们共用同一套电机，差别在握把。白色款是紧凑全能型；T 型够得到背和肩；环形握把有两种握法，方便换角度；专业款是给整天在用的理疗师做的。怎么选，看是谁用、要够到哪里。
Should we stock textured or smooth fascia balls?该备浮点筋膜球还是光面筋膜球？
Textured balls concentrate pressure into a smaller area and are the ones for trigger points. Smooth balls spread the load and work better over a broad area like the back or the thighs. The six-colour set is built for retail, so a customer can take one of each and find out which they prefer.浮点球把压力集中到更小的面积上，是打激痛点的；光面球把力摊开，在背部、大腿这种大面积上更好用。六色套装就是为零售做的，客户可以每样拿一个，自己试出偏好。
Which massage stick length should we stock?按摩棒该备哪个长度？
We build a 34 cm and a 32 cm stick, both on a PP and stainless-steel-pipe construction. The longer one gives more reach for calves and the lower back, and the shorter one fits a gym bag. Stocking both covers the two use cases from one order.我们做 34 cm 和 32 cm 两种，都是 PP 加不锈钢管的做法。长的一支够得到小腿和下背，短的能塞进健身包。两种都备，一张单就把两个场景覆盖了。
How are the fascia guns charged?筋膜枪怎么充电？
They take a rechargeable cell, and the exact type, run time and certification depend on the market you ship into. Confirm the destination and we will quote the version that clears import there, rather than a generic specification sheet.用可充电电池，具体型号、续航与认证取决于你的目的市场。确认销往哪里，我们报的是能在那边过关的版本，而不是发一份通用规格表。
What is the MOQ on a recovery kit?恢复套装的起订量是多少？
MOQ is 100 pcs per item and colour, with sampling in about 7 days. Guns, balls, sticks and clamps can be combined in one shipment in matched colours, which is how most studio and gym chains order a recovery kit.每个单品、每个颜色 100 片起订，约 7 天出样。筋膜枪、球、按摩棒与腿部夹可以并到同一批、配色统一，大多数瑜伽馆与连锁健身房订恢复包就是这么下的。
