EVA or TPE?

Searching for an EVA yoga mat?

In this trade the two names are used for the same mat. Buyers, studios and importers routinely ask for an “EVA yoga mat” when what they need is the closed-cell foam mat we produce in TPE — the same product, quoted under either word. So when your enquiry arrives as EVA, we quote and sample it as a TPE mat: MOQ from 100 pcs, sampling in about 7 days, the same printing, colour and packaging options.

EVA on its own we run for the accessory half of the order — high-density blocks and softer rollers, colour-matched to the mats. If your brief specifies EVA and you also want the TPE technical sheet, just ask and we send both.

Do you make EVA yoga mats?
Yes. In this trade an EVA yoga mat is how many buyers and studios ask for the TPE mat, so that is what we quote and sample: closed-cell TPE, MOQ from 100 pcs, samples in about 7 days. Our EVA-specific production is the accessory range — blocks and soft rollers.
Is an EVA yoga mat the same as a TPE yoga mat?
In this trade the two names are used for the same mat. EVA and TPE are different polymers, so our technical sheet states TPE — but an enquiry written as EVA yoga mat is quoted and sampled as our TPE mat, at the same MOQ and in the same price band.
FAQ

TPE yoga mat questions buyers ask

What is a TPE yoga mat made of?
Closed-cell thermoplastic elastomer foam — no phthalates, no latex and no PVC, and no rubber or plastic smell when the mat is unrolled. We compound and foam it in-house, so density and firmness are set to your brief rather than taken off a generic sheet.
Which sizes and thicknesses can you run?
Standard length is 183 cm, in widths of 61 cm, 66 cm, 80 cm and 90 cm, from 4 mm to 12 mm. The best-selling thicknesses are 6 mm and 8 mm. Wider formats up to 122 cm, and non-standard sizes, come off the same mould family, so they need a quotation but no new tooling.
Can the mat carry our own print, embossed logo and packaging?
Yes. Solid, dual-colour, spliced and camouflage surfaces, alignment lines, embossed or printed logos, printed carry straps, and retail packaging with your barcode — all in one production run. Send us the artwork and we confirm which effects survive repeated folding and cleaning before you commit.
What is the MOQ and lead time for a private-label TPE mat?
MOQ is 100 pcs per colour and design; samples with your logo take about 7 days, and repeat production runs 20 to 30 days from artwork sign-off. Colourways can be combined in a single order as long as each one meets the 100 pcs minimum.
What testing comes with a TPE mat order?
Testing is issued per material and colour for ROHS, CE and REACH, backed by ISO 9001 and BSCI audits at the plant and SGS third-party testing on request. We tell you which tests your market actually requires instead of sending a generic pack.
