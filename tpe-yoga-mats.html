EVA or TPE?EVA 还是 TPE？

Searching for an EVA yoga mat?在找 EVA 瑜伽垫？

In this trade the two names are used for the same mat. Buyers, studios and importers routinely ask for an “EVA yoga mat” when what they need is the closed-cell foam mat we produce in TPE — the same product, quoted under either word. So when your enquiry arrives as EVA, we quote and sample it as a TPE mat: MOQ from 100 pcs, sampling in about 7 days, the same printing, colour and packaging options.在这个行业里，这两个叫法指的就是同一张垫子。采购商、瑜伽馆和进口商常常开口就要“EVA 瑜伽垫”，而他们真正要的，正是我们用 TPE 生产的闭孔发泡垫——同一个产品，两个名字都能报。所以询盘以 EVA 名义进来时，我们就按 TPE 垫报价、打样：100 片起订、约 7 天出样，印刷、颜色和包装选项完全相同。

EVA on its own we run for the accessory half of the order — high-density blocks and softer rollers, colour-matched to the mats. If your brief specifies EVA and you also want the TPE technical sheet, just ask and we send both.EVA 本身，我们用它做订单里配件的那一半——高密度瑜伽砖与偏软的泡沫轴，与垫子同色系。如果规格书上写的是 EVA，而你也想要 TPE 的技术资料，说一声，两份都发。

Do you make EVA yoga mats?你们做 EVA 瑜伽垫吗？
Yes. In this trade an EVA yoga mat is how many buyers and studios ask for the TPE mat, so that is what we quote and sample: closed-cell TPE, MOQ from 100 pcs, samples in about 7 days. Our EVA-specific production is the accessory range — blocks and soft rollers.做。这个行业里，很多采购商和瑜伽馆就是用“EVA 瑜伽垫”来指 TPE 垫，所以我们按 TPE 报价、打样：闭孔 TPE，100 片起订，约 7 天出样。EVA 专线生产的是配件系列——瑜伽砖与软质泡沫轴。
Is an EVA yoga mat the same as a TPE yoga mat?EVA 瑜伽垫和 TPE 瑜伽垫是同一种吗？
In this trade the two names are used for the same mat. EVA and TPE are different polymers, so our technical sheet states TPE — but an enquiry written as EVA yoga mat is quoted and sampled as our TPE mat, at the same MOQ and in the same price band.在这个行业里，这两个叫法指的是同一张垫子。EVA 与 TPE 是两种不同的聚合物，所以我们的技术资料上写的是 TPE；但以 EVA 瑜伽垫名义发来的询盘，我们按 TPE 垫报价、打样，起订量与价格带完全一样。
FAQ常见问题

TPE yoga mat questions buyers ask买家会问的 TPE 瑜伽垫问题

What is a TPE yoga mat made of?TPE 瑜伽垫是什么材质做的？
Closed-cell thermoplastic elastomer foam — no phthalates, no latex and no PVC, and no rubber or plastic smell when the mat is unrolled. We compound and foam it in-house, so density and firmness are set to your brief rather than taken off a generic sheet.闭孔热塑性弹性体发泡材质 —— 不含邻苯类增塑剂、不含乳胶、不含 PVC，拆卷后也没有橡胶味或塑料味。发泡是我们自己车间做的，密度与软硬度按你的要求调，不是拿通用板材切。
Which sizes and thicknesses can you run?你们能做哪些尺寸和厚度？
Standard length is 183 cm, in widths of 61 cm, 66 cm, 80 cm and 90 cm, from 4 mm to 12 mm. The best-selling thicknesses are 6 mm and 8 mm. Wider formats up to 122 cm, and non-standard sizes, come off the same mould family, so they need a quotation but no new tooling.标准长度 183 cm，宽度 61 cm、66 cm、80 cm、90 cm，厚度 4 mm 到 12 mm，其中最好卖的是 6 mm 与 8 mm。最宽可做到 122 cm；非标尺寸与常规规格共用同一套模具，所以只需要报价，不需要重新开模。
Can the mat carry our own print, embossed logo and packaging?能印我们自己的图案、压印 Logo 和包装吗？
Yes. Solid, dual-colour, spliced and camouflage surfaces, alignment lines, embossed or printed logos, printed carry straps, and retail packaging with your barcode — all in one production run. Send us the artwork and we confirm which effects survive repeated folding and cleaning before you commit.可以。纯色、双色、拼色与迷彩表面，体位线，压印或印刷 Logo，印花背带，带条码的零售包装 —— 一次生产就能全做完。把设计稿发来，我们会先告诉你哪些效果经得起反复折叠和清洁，再让你决定。
What is the MOQ and lead time for a private-label TPE mat?贴牌 TPE 垫的起订量和交期是多少？
MOQ is 100 pcs per colour and design; samples with your logo take about 7 days, and repeat production runs 20 to 30 days from artwork sign-off. Colourways can be combined in a single order as long as each one meets the 100 pcs minimum.起订量 100 片／颜色与款式；带 Logo 的样品约 7 天；翻单生产在确认设计稿后 20 到 30 天。多个颜色可以并到一张订单，只要每个颜色各满 100 片。
What testing comes with a TPE mat order?下单会附哪些检测？
Testing is issued per material and colour for ROHS, CE and REACH, backed by ISO 9001 and BSCI audits at the plant and SGS third-party testing on request. We tell you which tests your market actually requires instead of sending a generic pack.按材质与颜色出具 ROHS、CE 与 REACH 检测报告，工厂有 ISO 9001 与 BSCI 审核背书，可按需做 SGS 第三方检测。我们会告诉你你的目标市场到底需要哪些测试，而不是发一份通用资料包了事。
