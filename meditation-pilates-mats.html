Meditation and Pilates Guide

Meditation, mandala and reformer mat builds

This family covers three jobs a standard yoga mat does not do. Round meditation mats give a defined seat for sitting practice, in a 68 cm round or a 40 × 60 cm rectangle rather than a 183 cm roll. Bolsters support the knees and spine in restorative work, and the ones we build are 64 × 24 × 13 cm at 550 g, so they ship in a normal parcel. Mandala and gradient prints are what turn a functional cushion into a retail gift.

Pilates reformer mats are the technical part, and they are made to order. Every machine has a different carriage, shoulder pad and foot-bar layout, so we cut to your drawing and add your logo rather than forcing you into a stock shape. Send the machine model, or the dimensions and the position of the logo, and the first sample is the confirmation step.

Compared with a yoga mat these are shorter and denser, and the material follows the job. A firm PU surface over rubber suits seated practice. Where landed cost matters more, TPE brings the weight down. Suede comes in when the artwork carries the design. Colour and artwork are set per order across the whole set, so a programme ships as a matched kit. MOQ is 100 pcs, samples in about 7 days.

FAQ

Meditation and pilates mat questions buyers ask

How is a meditation mat different from a yoga mat?
It is shorter and denser. A meditation mat is built to give a stable seat, so the formats are a 40 × 60 cm rectangle or a 68 cm round rather than a 183 cm roll, and the surface is firmer than a yoga mat of the same material. The cushioning is sized for sitting, not for standing poses.
Which round sizes do you run?
The round mandala build is 68 cm across, and the rectangular meditation mats run 40 × 60 cm in both PU over rubber and TPE. Custom diameters and rectangles come off the same tooling, so a non-standard size needs a quotation rather than a new mould.
How do we specify a reformer mat?
Send the machine model, or a photo with the carriage, the shoulder pads and the foot bar measured, plus where the logo should sit. Reformer mats are cut to your drawing, so the first sample is the confirmation step and no stock shape is imposed on you.
Can the mandala artwork be changed?
Yes. Mandala, tropical and gradient artwork are all printed to your file, and a custom logo can be combined with it. Send the artwork and we will confirm how it reproduces on the surface you have chosen before you commit.
What is the MOQ on a mixed programme?
MOQ is 100 pcs per design, with sampling in about 7 days. Mats, bolsters and round cushions can be combined in one shipment, and because colour and artwork are set per order they can be matched across the whole set.
