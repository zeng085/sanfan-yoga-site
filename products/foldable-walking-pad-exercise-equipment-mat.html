Product Overview

Foldable Walking Pad & Exercise Equipment Floor Mat

A large, flat protective mat for the home-gym corner. It sits under a walking pad, treadmill, exercise bike or rowing machine, shielding hardwood, laminate and tile from scuffs and spreading the load, while the foam layer dampens vibration and footfall noise. Pressed fold lines let it fold down to about 20 × 30 cm, so it ships in a small carton and stores easily when the machine is moved.

Key Features

  • NBR, TPE or EVA foam — material chosen to suit your price point
  • Reference size approx. 140 × 60 cm, about 6 mm thick
  • Pressed fold lines — folds to approx. 20 × 30 cm for compact shipping and storage
  • Embossed surface texture; custom colour, logo and packaging per order

FAQ

Foldable Walking Pad & Exercise Equipment Floor Mat — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
For TPE the minimum is 100 pcs per design and colour, the same low MOQ we apply to TPE products. For NBR and EVA versions the minimum is confirmed on request against your size and thickness.
Can this be made under our own brand?
Yes. OEM and ODM are our default mode. Send your logo artwork, material choice, size, colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in about 7 days for your approval before the bulk run.
What is the lead time and price?
Bulk production takes 15–20 days after sample approval; we confirm the exact timeline at quotation. Price is quoted on request against material, size, thickness, logo and packaging. We reply within 2 hours on working days.
Which material should I choose — NBR, TPE or EVA?
All three are foam materials we work with daily. Tell us the machine the mat goes under, your target retail price and the feel you want, and we recommend a material and send samples so you can compare before ordering.
