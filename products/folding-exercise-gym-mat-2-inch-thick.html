Product Overview

2-Inch Thick Folding Exercise Gym Mat (Multi-Panel, with Carry Handles)

Folding panel mats give a thick, even surface for floor exercise without taking up permanent floor space. This one is 2 inches thick, with a high-density foam core inside a wipeable PVC or PU cover. The mat is divided into panels joined along fold seams, so it opens out flat for stretching, planks, sit-ups, home workouts and beginner tumbling, then folds into a compact block and is carried by its handles to a cupboard, car boot or studio corner. The reference size is 72 × 24 × 2 in; length, width, panel count, foam density, cover material, colour and logo are built to your specification and confirmed with your quote and sample.
