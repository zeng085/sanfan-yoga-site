Product Overview

Fabric Hip Resistance Band Set (3 Levels: 60 / 90 / 120 LB)

Fabric hip bands sit around the thighs or just above the knees for glute bridges, squats, side steps, clamshells and Pilates leg work. Woven polyester-cotton with latex thread gives a wide, soft band that stays in place on the leg rather than rolling the way thin latex loops can. The set steps up in three levels — Light 60 LB, Medium 90 LB, Heavy 120 LB — so one pack covers warm-ups through heavier lower-body work. The set weighs 355 g and is shown packed with a storage pouch in an OPP bag. Colour, logo/label and packaging are made to your brand; MOQ, sample and lead time are confirmed with your quote.
