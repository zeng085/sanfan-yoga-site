FAQ

EVA Massage Rollers — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for EVA Massage Rollers is 500 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.
Can this be manufactured under our own brand?
Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for EVA Massage Rollers. Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
What can be customized?
All of the following are made to order on this item: Logo: Customization Available. Colors & Print: Custom colors & hardness. Packaging: Shrink wrap + carton. Material: EVA.
Can the size and thickness be changed?
Standard build is 34×12.5 cm / 33×13.5 cm / 52×12 cm, Ø 12.5 / 13.5 cm thick. Non-standard sizes and thicknesses are produced to order — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility, tooling (if any) and the minimum for that build.
Is it compliant for the US and EU?
EVA Massage Rollers is made in our ISO 9001:2015-certified factory, which was amfori BSCI audited in 2025 (renewal in progress). Material test reports for your target market (for example REACH or California Prop 65) can be arranged on request for your order.
