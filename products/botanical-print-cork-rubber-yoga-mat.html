Product Overview

Botanical Print Cork Rubber Yoga Mat植物印花软木橡胶垫

A natural cork veneer bonded to a rubber base. Cork grips better the damper it gets, so it suits hot and sweaty practice, and it carries no plastic feel — the finish most often asked for by sustainability-led brands.天然软木面层复合橡胶底。软木遇汗越湿越涩，适合高温出汗的练习场景，且没有塑料触感，是主打可持续定位的品牌最常点的面料。
