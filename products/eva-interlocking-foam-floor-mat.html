Product Overview

What the EVA interlocking floor mat is

The EVA interlocking floor mat is a set of square foam tiles with puzzle-cut edges that lock together into one continuous floor, so a buyer can cover a small workout corner or a whole room and add tiles later. Straight border strips clip onto the outer edges to close off the puzzle teeth and give a neat finished line. For gym use the key point is density: a higher-density EVA keeps its thickness under racks, benches, treadmills and dropped dumbbells instead of denting, and a thicker 1 in tile gives more cushioning than the standard 1/2 in. The textured underside grips hard floors so the tiles do not creep apart during workouts, while the top can be a fine embossed texture for grip or a wood-grain print for a living-room look.

FAQ

EVA Interlocking Foam Floor Mat — buyer questions

What size and thickness are the tiles?
Each tile is about 24 × 24 in (61 × 61 cm). The standard thickness is 1/2 in (about 12 mm); 1 in (about 25 mm) and other thicknesses can be made on request. Straight border strips are supplied for the outer edges, and the tile count per set is chosen by you.
Will the tiles dent or shift under gym equipment?
Denting depends mainly on foam density and thickness, so we set a higher-density EVA for heavier equipment and suggest the 1 in tile where more cushioning is needed. The textured underside grips hard floors so the tiles stay locked together. We recommend testing a sample with your own equipment before mass production.
What surface and colour options are available?
The top can be a fine embossed texture or a wood-grain print, and the underside has a textured anti-slip pattern. Tiles are made in your brand colours, as single-colour or mixed sets, with a printed or embossed logo confirmed at quote.
What is the minimum order quantity?
The minimum order, price and lead time are confirmed at quote, because they depend on thickness, density, surface, colour and packing. Tooling for tiles and border strips is also confirmed at quote. Tell us your target quantity and spec and we reply with the details.
How long does a sample take?
A sample takes about 7 days once the size, thickness, surface and colour are agreed. The sample cost is confirmed at quote.
