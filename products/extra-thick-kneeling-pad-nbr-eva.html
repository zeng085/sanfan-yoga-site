Product Overview

Extra-Thick Kneeling Pad (NBR / EVA)

A thick, portable cushion for anything done on the knees. In yoga and Pilates it goes under the knees in lunges, table-top and kneeling poses where a mat alone is too thin; at home it softens floor workouts; outdoors it doubles as a garden kneeler. The cut-out handle makes it easy to carry and hang, and the embossed surface helps it stay put. Feel can be tuned by choosing NBR or EVA, or a two-layer build with a softer top.

Key Features

  • Extra-thick NBR or EVA foam for knee cushioning
  • Optional two-layer build with a softer top layer over a firmer base
