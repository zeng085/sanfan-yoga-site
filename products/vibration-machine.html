FAQ

Vibration Plate — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for Vibration Plate is 500 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.
Can this be manufactured under our own brand?
Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for Vibration Plate. Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
What can be customized?
Material, size, thickness, colour, logo and packaging are all produced to your specification — send the spec you want and we build to it.
Can the size and thickness be changed?
Sizes, thicknesses and colours are all produced to order on this item — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility, tooling (if any) and the minimum for that build.
Is it compliant for the US and EU?
Vibration Plate is made in our ISO 9001:2015-certified factory, which was amfori BSCI audited in 2025 (renewal in progress). Material test reports for your target market (for example REACH or California Prop 65) can be arranged on request for your order.
