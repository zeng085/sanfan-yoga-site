Product Overview

What the barbell squat and hip thrust pad is

The barbell pad is a thick, high-density foam sleeve that sits on the middle of the bar. A lengthwise centre slot lets it open and wrap the shaft without removing the weight plates, and it is sized for standard Olympic bars. In back squats and lunges it spreads the bar across a wider area of the upper back for a more comfortable hold; in hip thrusts and glute bridges it sits across the hips. Two things keep it in place: the slot grips the bar, and optional hook-and-loop straps close the pad so it does not roll or slide off. NBR gives a softer, denser cushion; EVA is lighter and firmer. The outer surface carries an embossed anti-slip texture.

FAQ

Barbell Squat & Hip Thrust Pad — buyer questions

Will the pad fit my barbell?
The pad is sized for the shaft of standard Olympic barbells. If your bar has a different shaft diameter, tell us the size and we confirm the slot and inner diameter at quote.
What is the difference between the NBR and EVA versions?
NBR foam is softer and denser, giving a thicker cushion. EVA foam is lighter and firmer and comes in a wide range of colours. Both have an embossed anti-slip surface, and the density is chosen per order.
How does the pad stay in place on the bar?
The centre slot grips the bar, and optional hook-and-loop (velcro) straps close the pad around it so it does not roll or slide during squats or hip thrusts. The embossed outer surface also helps it stay put on the body.
What is the minimum order quantity?
The minimum order, price and lead time are confirmed at quote, because they depend on material, size, straps, colour and packing. Slot and mould tooling is also confirmed at quote. Tell us your target quantity and spec and we reply with the details.
How long does a sample take?
A sample takes about 7 days once the material, size, colour and artwork are agreed. The sample cost is confirmed at quote.
