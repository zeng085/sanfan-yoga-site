Product Overview

Flamingo Print Suede Rubber Yoga Mat

A microfibre suede face over a rubber or TPE base. Suede takes edge-to-edge digital printing with no visible pattern seam, which makes it the standard choice for licensed artwork, seasonal collections and gift lines.

FAQ

Flamingo Print Suede Rubber Yoga Mat — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for Flamingo Print Suede Rubber Yoga Mat is 300 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.
Can this be manufactured under our own brand?
Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for Flamingo Print Suede Rubber Yoga Mat. Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
What can be customized?
All of the following are made to order on this item: Logo: Customization Available. Colors: Multiple Color Options. Packaging: Poly Bag+Carton or customized. Material: Suede+Rubber.
Can the size and thickness be changed?
Standard build is 185x68cm, 1.5mm, customizable thick. Non-standard sizes and thicknesses are produced to order — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility, tooling (if any) and the minimum for that build.
Is it compliant for the US and EU?
Flamingo Print Suede Rubber Yoga Mat is made in our ISO 9001:2015-certified factory, which was amfori BSCI audited in 2025 (renewal in progress). Our TPE, PU rubber and suede rubber mats are RoHS 2.0 tested and our printed PU/rubber mat is California Prop 65 tested; full reports are available on request.
