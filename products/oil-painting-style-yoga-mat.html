Product Overview

Oil Painting Style Yoga Mat

A PU surface layer bonded to a natural rubber base — the combination behind high sweat absorption and a stable, non-slip ride. It is the flagship mat of most studio and premium retail lines, and takes printed, embossed or solid finishes.

FAQ

Oil Painting Style Yoga Mat — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for Oil Painting Style Yoga Mat is 300 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.
Can this be manufactured under our own brand?
Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for Oil Painting Style Yoga Mat. Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
What can be customized?
All of the following are made to order on this item: Logo: Customization Available. Colors: Field Poetry or Customized. Packaging: Poly Bag+Carton or customized. Material: PU+Rubber.
Can the size and thickness be changed?
Standard build is 185x68cm, 4.5mm / 6mm, customizable thick. Non-standard sizes and thicknesses are produced to order — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility, tooling (if any) and the minimum for that build.
Is it compliant for the US and EU?
Oil Painting Style Yoga Mat is made in our ISO 9001:2015-certified factory, which was amfori BSCI audited in 2025 (renewal in progress). Our TPE, PU rubber and suede rubber mats are RoHS 2.0 tested and our printed PU/rubber mat is California Prop 65 tested; full reports are available on request.
