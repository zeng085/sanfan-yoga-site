Product Overview

Natural Cork Yoga Block (Single or 2-Pack)

Cork blocks are the natural-material step up from foam: firm and stable under hand or hip, with a dry grip that suits sweaty hands, and a warm look that sits well beside a cork or rubber mat. A 2-pack is the format most buyers list, because many standing and seated poses use one block under each hand. Laser engraving burns your logo or a pattern straight into the cork, so the branding cannot peel off. Exact size, weight and density are confirmed with your quote and sample.

FAQ

Natural Cork Yoga Block (Single or 2-Pack) — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for the cork yoga block is confirmed at quote, because it depends on size, single or 2-pack, engraving and packaging. Tell us your quantity and we reply with the minimum and price.
Can you laser-engrave our logo?
Yes. Your logo or a decorative pattern can be laser-engraved into the cork. Send your artwork and we confirm the engraving size and position with a sample before the bulk run.
What size and weight is the block?
Common market sizes are 9 × 6 × 4 in (approx. 23 × 15 × 10 cm) and 9 × 6 × 3 in. We make the size you specify; exact dimensions, weight and density are confirmed with the quotation and sample.
Cork or EVA — which block should I choose?
Cork is firmer, heavier and has a natural look and dry grip; EVA foam is lighter, softer and available in many colours. Many brands offer both. We also make an EVA yoga block and can quote both together.
