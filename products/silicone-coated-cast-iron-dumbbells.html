Product Overview

Silicone-Coated Cast Iron Dumbbells (1 kg / 1.5 kg / 2 kg, Pair)

Light dumbbells are a staple of Pilates, barre and toning classes, where the arms work through many controlled repetitions. These have a cast iron core under a soft silicone coating with a smooth capsule shape, which is easy to grip and gentle on floors and studio equipment. Three weights step up evenly: 1 kg (15.5 × 4.2 cm), 1.5 kg (18.6 × 4.5 cm) and 2 kg (22.0 × 4.8 cm), each sold as a pair in a box. Colour, logo and packaging are made to your brand; MOQ, sample and lead time are confirmed with your quote.
