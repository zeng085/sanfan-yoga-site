Product Overview

TPE Foam Balance Pad

A thick, soft TPE foam pad that gives an unstable standing surface for balance work and a cushioned base for knees and wrists. Closed-cell TPE foam does not soak up sweat and wipes clean; both faces carry a fine textured pattern for grip on the floor and under bare feet.

Key Features

  • TPE closed-cell foam — soft, cushioned and easy to wipe clean
  • Standard approx. 39 × 32 × 5.7 cm, XL 48 × 38 × 6.4 cm
  • Textured non-slip pattern on both sides
  • Custom colour, logo and retail packaging per order

FAQ

TPE Foam Balance Pad — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for the TPE Foam Balance Pad is 100 pcs per design and colour, the same low MOQ we apply to TPE products, so a new brand can test the market with one colour first.
Can this be made under our own brand?
Yes. OEM and ODM are our default mode. Send your logo artwork, target colour, size and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in about 7 days for your approval before the bulk run.
What is the lead time and price?
Bulk production takes 15–20 days after sample approval; we confirm the exact timeline at quotation. Price is quoted on request against your size, colour, logo and packaging. We reply within 2 hours on working days.
Which sizes are available?
Standard size is approx. 39 × 32 × 5.7 cm and the XL size approx. 48 × 38 × 6.4 cm. Other dimensions can be made to order — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility and the minimum for that build.
