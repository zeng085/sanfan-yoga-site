Product Overview

Yoga Wheel with Cork or TPE Cover

A curved prop that does what a block cannot: under the upper back it opens the chest and supports a gentle backbend, rolled along the spine it gives a guided stretch, and under hands or feet it adds a balance challenge in yoga and Pilates. The outer cover is where comfort and grip come from, and that is our core material — cork for a natural look and dry grip, or TPE foam for a softer, cushioned feel in custom colours. It pairs naturally with a cork or TPE mat and block in the same range.

FAQ

Yoga Wheel with Cork or TPE Cover — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for the yoga wheel is confirmed on request, because it depends on cork or TPE cover, single wheel or 3-size set, colours and packaging. Tell us your quantity and we reply with the minimum and price.
Can this be made under our own brand?
Yes. OEM and ODM are our default mode. Send your logo artwork, cover material, sizes, colours and packaging, and we arrange a physical sample for your approval before the bulk run.
How much weight can the wheel take?
The load rating depends on the inner ring chosen for your order. We confirm the core material and rated load with the quotation and sample, so you can state it accurately on your listing.
Cork or TPE — which cover should I choose?
Cork gives a natural look and a dry, firm grip; TPE foam is softer, lighter on the back and available in more colours. If you already sell a cork or TPE mat, matching the wheel cover to it keeps the range consistent. We can send both for comparison.
