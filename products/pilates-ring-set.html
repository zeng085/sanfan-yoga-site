Product Overview

What is in the set

The set puts three small Pilates tools in one box, so a buyer lists one product instead of three. The ring is 14 inches (36 cm) across, built on a fiberglass core with a foam, PVC or rubber outer cover and two padded EVA handles for inner-thigh, arm and core work. The 9-inch mini Pilates ball is made in anti-burst PVC and comes with an inflation straw. The loop resistance bands are TPE, with the number of bands and their colours set per order. Every part is built to your colours and logo, and the whole set is packed in a box or bag in your design.

FAQ

Pilates Ring Set — buyer questions

What comes in the Pilates ring set?
A 14-inch (36 cm) Pilates ring with a fiberglass core, a foam, PVC or rubber outer cover and two padded EVA handles; a 9-inch (about 23 cm) mini Pilates ball; and loop resistance bands. The number of bands and the mix of components are set per order.
What is the minimum order quantity for the set?
The minimum for the set is confirmed at quote, because it depends on the components, colours and packaging you choose. Tell us your target quantity and set contents and we reply with the minimum.
Can the set carry our own brand?
Yes. Your logo can go on the ring handles, the mini ball and the bands, and the set can ship in a printed box or bag in your design. Ring cover colour, ball colour and band colours are set per order.
How long does a sample take?
A sample set takes about 7 days once the specification and artwork are agreed. The sample cost is confirmed at quote.
Can we change what goes in the set?
Yes. You can choose the ring cover material, the number of loop bands and their colours, or build a smaller set such as ring and ball only. Resistance levels, set weight and carton size are confirmed at quote for the build you choose.
