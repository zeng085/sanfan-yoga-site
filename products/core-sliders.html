Product Overview

Dual-Sided Core Sliders (2-Pack)

Core sliders turn the floor into a low-impact training tool: hands or feet rest on the discs and slide through mountain climbers, reverse lunges, pikes and Pilates-style core work. Because homes have different floors, each disc has two faces — EVA foam for hard floors and smooth plastic for carpet — so one pair works in a living room, a bedroom or a studio. The plain round shape stacks flat and packs small, which suits online listings and studio retail. Diameter is offered in common 7 in or 9 in reference sizes; the final size, thickness, weight and bonding process are confirmed with your quote and sample.
