Product Overview

Stackable EVA Squat Wedge & Slant Board Set

A simple, light incline prop for the gym floor, studio or living room. Under the heels it supports deeper, more upright squats; under the toes it gives a steady calf and hamstring stretch; stacked, it gives a steeper incline from the same set. The EVA foam is the same material we use for our yoga blocks, so it is easy to colour-match a wedge set to an existing block, mat or Pilates range.

Key Features

  • Dense EVA foam — the material of our EVA yoga blocks
  • Sold as a pair or as a stacking set — stack wedges for a steeper incline
  • Anti-slip embossed texture on the incline surface
  • Custom size, angle, colour, logo and packaging per order

FAQ

Stackable EVA Squat Wedge & Slant Board Set — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
For EVA foam props such as our EVA yoga blocks the minimum is 500 pcs, and we apply the same starting point to the wedge set. The final minimum is confirmed at quotation against your size, angle, colour and packaging.
Can this be made under our own brand?
Yes. OEM and ODM are our default mode. Send your logo artwork, size and angle, colours and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in about 7 days for your approval before the bulk run.
What is the lead time and price?
Bulk production takes 15–20 days after sample approval; we confirm the exact timeline at quotation. Price is quoted on request. We reply within 2 hours on working days.
Which sizes and angles can you make?
Sizes and angles are made to order. Tell us the footprint, height or angle you want, and whether you need a pair or a stacking set, and we cut a sample to that specification for you to test.
