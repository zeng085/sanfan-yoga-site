Pilates Bar Kit with Resistance Bands

A compact Pilates bar kit for full-body home workouts. The bar breaks down into threaded steel sections for storage and shipping, foam grips keep hands comfortable, and the swivel hooks let the bands turn freely. Each latex tube band runs inside a woven nylon sleeve that helps contain the band if it snaps.

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for the Pilates Bar Kit is 1,000 pcs, in line with our resistance band products. Logo, colour and packaging are set on the same run.
Can this be made under our own brand?
Yes. OEM and ODM are our default mode. Send your logo artwork, colours, band set and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in about 7 days for your approval before the bulk run.
What is the lead time and price?
Bulk production takes 15–20 days after sample approval; we confirm the exact timeline at quotation. Price is quoted on request against your kit make-up and packaging. We reply within 2 hours on working days.
