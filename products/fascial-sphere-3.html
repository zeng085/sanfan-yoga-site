FAQ

Fascial Sphere Set (6 Colours) — buyer questions

What is the minimum order quantity?
Minimum order quantity for Fascial Sphere Set (6 Colours) is 500 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.
Can this be manufactured under our own brand?
Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for Fascial Sphere Set (6 Colours). Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
What can be customized?
Material, size, thickness, colour, logo and packaging are all produced to your specification — send the spec you want and we build to it.
Can the size and thickness be changed?
Sizes, thicknesses and colours are all produced to order on this item — send your target specification and we confirm feasibility, tooling (if any) and the minimum for that build.
