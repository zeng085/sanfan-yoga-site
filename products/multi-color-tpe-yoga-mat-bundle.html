Product Overview

Multi-Color TPE Yoga Mat Bundle

Made from double-layer eco TPE that is lightweight, recyclable and free of PVC and toxic odours. Both faces are textured for grip, and the range covers single-colour and dual-colour constructions so a full width and thickness ladder can sit under one brand.

Key Features

  • Eco TPE — no PVC and no odour
  • Textured non-slip surface on both faces
  • Widths 61–122 cm and thickness 3–20 mm, both customizable

Minimum order quantity for Multi-Color TPE Yoga Mat Bundle is 100 pcs. A first order at that quantity is enough to test a market, with your own logo, colour and packaging set on the same run.

Yes — OEM and ODM are the default mode for Multi-Color TPE Yoga Mat Bundle. Send your logo artwork, target colour and packaging, and we produce a physical sample in 7 days so you can approve it before the bulk run.
