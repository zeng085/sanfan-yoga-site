Product Overview

What the yoga foam wedge is

The yoga foam wedge is a long, slim prop that sits across the front of the mat. At about 20 inches (51 cm) long it fits under both hands or both heels at once, and its low slope — from 2 inches (5 cm) down to a thin edge — supports a comfortable wrist angle in plank, downward dog and other weight-bearing poses. Turned around, it lifts the heels for squatting poses and standing work in yoga and Pilates classes. The standard width is about 6 inches (15 cm); a wider build of about 8–9 inches (20–23 cm) gives more room for the whole palm or foot. The EVA version is light, with a slight give, and its density can be set softer or firmer; the cork version is firmer, heavier and natural in tone.

FAQ

Yoga Foam Wedge — buyer questions

What size is the yoga foam wedge?
The standard wedge is about 20 × 6 × 2 in (51 × 15 × 5 cm): long enough to sit under both hands or both heels across the mat, and sloping from 2 in (5 cm) down to a thin edge. A wider version of about 8–9 in (20–23 cm) can be made, and other lengths or heights are confirmed at quote.
What is the difference between the EVA and cork versions?
The EVA wedge is high-density closed-cell foam: light, with a slight give, and available in many colours and firmness levels. The cork wedge is natural agglomerated cork: firmer and heavier, with a warm natural look, and it can be laser-engraved with your logo.
How is it used in yoga and Pilates?
Placed under the hands, the slope supports a comfortable wrist angle in plank, downward dog and other weight-bearing poses. Placed under the heels, it lifts them for squatting poses and standing work. Teachers also use it under the hips or knees as a low prop.
