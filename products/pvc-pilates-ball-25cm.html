Product Overview

PVC Pilates Ball 25 cm (with Inflation Straw & Plug)

The 25 cm Pilates ball is the small, soft ball used in mat Pilates, barre and core classes: squeezed between the knees in bridges, placed behind the lower back for supported roll-downs, or held between the hands for arm work. It ships flat-ish and light at 100 g, and the user inflates it to the firmness they like with the included straw, then seals it with the air plug. Packed one per OPP bag, it suits studio retail, subscription boxes and online listings. Colour, logo and packaging are made to your brand; MOQ, sample and lead time are confirmed with your quote.
