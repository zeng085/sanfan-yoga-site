FAQ常见问题

PU rubber yoga mat questions buyers ask买家会问的 PU 橡胶瑜伽垫问题

How is a PU rubber yoga mat built?PU 橡胶瑜伽垫是怎么做成的？
A natural rubber base bonded to a moisture-absorbing PU top layer. The rubber stops the mat sliding on the floor and provides the cushioning; the PU gives the dry, matte hand-feel and the grip. It is the construction behind most premium studio and hot-yoga mats.天然橡胶底贴合吸湿 PU 面层。橡胶负责不在地上打滑、提供缓冲；PU 负责干爽磨砂的手感与防滑。绝大多数高端馆用垫与高温瑜伽垫都是这个结构。
Why choose PU rubber over TPE for hot yoga?高温瑜伽为什么选 PU 橡胶而不是 TPE？
Because PU grips better once it is wet, which is exactly the problem in a heated class — TPE gets slippery. For general retail at a lower price point, TPE is usually the better value. Tell us the class type and the retail price band and we recommend one rather than quote both.因为 PU 越湿越防滑，而这正好是高温教室的核心问题 —— TPE 出汗后会打滑。如果是一般零售、价格带更低，TPE 通常性价比更好。告诉我们课程类型与零售价带，我们只推荐一种，不会两份都报。
What thickness and size options are there?厚度和尺寸有哪些选择？
Thickness options start at 1.5 mm for travel and ultra-thin studio mats, and go up to 5 mm with cushioning. The common formats are 183 × 68 cm, 185 × 68 cm and 181 × 66 cm, and size, shape and corners are customizable. Suede-feel, matte and print-ready surfaces are all available.最薄 1.5 mm，用于旅行与超薄馆用垫；带缓冲可做到 5 mm。常用规格为 183 × 68 cm、185 × 68 cm、181 × 66 cm，尺寸、形状与圆角均可定制。绒感、磨砂与可印花表面都能做。
How do we look after a PU rubber mat?PU 橡胶垫平时怎么保养？
Wipe it with water and a pH-neutral cleaner, dry it flat, and keep it out of a hot car. Heat and detergent are what break the PU layer down; practice does not. Looked after properly, expect two to three years of daily studio use.用清水加 pH 中性的清洁剂擦拭，平摊晾干，别放在暴晒的车里。让 PU 面层老化的是高温和洗涤剂，不是练习。保养得当，天天上课的馆用垫可以用两到三年。
What is the MOQ, and can you match our Pantone colour?起订量多少？能匹配我们的 Pantone 色号吗？
MOQ is 100 pcs per colour and design, samples in about 7 days, and production 25 to 35 days after sign-off. PU rubber needs a longer cure than TPE, so book the launch window accordingly. Pantone matching on the PU top layer, debossed or printed logos and custom packaging are all standard.起订量 100 片／颜色与款式，约 7 天出样，确认设计稿后 25 到 35 天生产 —— PU 橡胶的固化时间比 TPE 长，排上市档期要留出这段。PU 面层可对 Pantone 色号，压印或印刷 Logo、定制包装都是标准配置。
